Cadre Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

​

Grew Quarterly Net Sales 30% and Net Income 46% Year-Over-Year

​

Increased Quarterly Adjusted EBITDA of $41.1 Million and Expanded Adjusted EBITDA Margin of 20.2%

​

Raises Full-Year 2026 Guidance for Second Consecutive Quarter

​

Announces $100 Million Inaugural Stock Repurchase Program

​

Targets Further Operating-Margin Expansion Through the Cadre Operating Model and AI Initiatives

​

JACKSONVILLE, Fla., November 4, 2026 – Cadre Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: CDRE) (“Cadre” or “Company”), a global leader in the manufacturing and distribution of safety equipment and other related products for the law enforcement, first responder, military and nuclear markets, announced today its consolidated operating results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

​

●Net sales of $202.9 million for the third quarter; net sales of $565.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
●Gross profit margin of 43.4% for the third quarter; gross profit margin of 41.6% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
●Net income of $16.0 million, or $0.37 per diluted share, for the third quarter; net income of $29.4 million, or $0.68 per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
●Adjusted EBITDA of $41.1 million for the third quarter; Adjusted EBITDA of $104.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
●Adjusted EBITDA margin of 20.2% for the third quarter; Adjusted EBITDA margin of 18.4% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.
●Declared quarterly cash dividend of $0.10 per share in October 2026.

​

"Our third-quarter results reflect strong execution across Cadre’s businesses, with double-digit organic growth and contributions from acquisitions driving a 30% increase in sales,” said Warren Kanders, CEO and Chairman. “Net income increased 46% and Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 110 basis points to 20.2%. We remain focused on driving further operating-margin improvement through the Cadre Operating Model, with an emphasis on manufacturing productivity, sourcing and pricing discipline, and overhead efficiency. Our AI initiatives will complement these efforts by targeting repetitive workflows, improving decision-making and enabling our teams to scale more efficiently.”

​

Mr. Kanders added, “Heightened geopolitical uncertainty, evolving public-safety requirements and investment in nuclear infrastructure create enduring demand drivers for our mission-critical products. Against this backdrop, healthy customer demand and another quarter of better-than-expected results support our decision to again raise full-year guidance. We will continue to pursue complementary acquisitions and apply our operating model across both existing and newly acquired businesses to drive profitable growth, stronger cash generation and long-term shareholder value. Our share repurchase program announced today reflects our disciplined approach to capital allocation.”

​

Third Quarter and Nine-Month 2026 Operating Results

​

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Cadre generated net sales of $202.9 million, as compared to $155.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. This increase was primarily a result of current-year acquisitions and increased demand for EOD products.

​

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Cadre generated net sales of $565.4 million, as compared to $443.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, mainly driven by current- and prior- year acquisitions and higher demand for EOD, crowd control and chemiluminescent products.

​

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Cadre generated gross profit of $88.1 million, as compared to $66.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Cadre generated gross profit of $235.4 million, as compared to $187.0 million for the prior year period.

​

Gross profit margin was 43.4% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 42.7% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, mainly driven by favorable pricing, partially offset by unfavorable operating leverage. Gross profit margin was 41.6% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 42.2% for the prior year period.

​

Net income was $16.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to net income of $10.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily a result of increased gross profit, partially offset by acquisition operating expenses, increased tax expense, and increased compensation expense.

​

Net income was $29.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to net income of $32.4 million for the prior year period, primarily as a result of acquisition operating expenses, increased compensation expense, interest expense, and adverse foreign currency fluctuations, partially offset by increased gross profit.

​

Cadre generated $41.1 million of Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $29.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.2% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 19.1% for the prior year period.

​

Cadre generated $104.2 million of Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $77.3 million for the prior period. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 18.4% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to 17.4% for the prior year period.

​

Product segment gross profit margin was 43.7% and 42.3% for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to 43.9% and 43.3% for the prior year periods.

​

Distribution segment gross profit margin was 23.4% and 22.1% for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to 21.3% and 22.0% for the prior year periods.

​

Liquidity, Cash Flows and Capital Allocation

​

●Cash and cash equivalents decreased by $59.0 million from $122.9 million as of December 31, 2025 to $63.9 million as of September 30, 2026.
●Total debt increased by $62.2 million from $307.3 million as of December 31, 2025 to $369.5 million as of September 30, 2026.
●Net debt (total debt net of cash and cash equivalents) increased by $121.2 million from $184.4 million as of December 31, 2025 to $305.6 million as of September 30, 2026.
●Capital expenditures totaled $2.9 million for the third quarter and $9.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $0.6 million for the third quarter and $3.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Dividend

​

On October 20, 2026, the Company announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.10 per share, or $0.40 per share on an annualized basis. Cadre's dividend payment will be made on November 13, 2026 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on the record date of October 30, 2026. The declaration of any future dividend is subject to the discretion of the Company's Board of Directors.

Increased 2026 Outlook

​

Cadre increased its full-year guidance and expects to generate net sales in 2026 of between $756 million and $769 million and Adjusted EBITDA in 2026 of between $140 million and $144 million. We expect capital expenditures to be in the range of $11 million to $14 million. Cadre has not provided net income guidance due to the inherent difficulty of forecasting, without unreasonable effort, certain types of expenses and gains, which affect net income but not Adjusted EBITDA. Therefore, we do not provide a reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA guidance to net income guidance.

About Cadre

​

Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, Cadre is a global leader in the manufacturing and distribution of safety products. Cadre's equipment provides critical protection to allow users to safely and securely perform their duties and protect those around them in hazardous or life-threatening situations. The Company's core products include body armor, explosive ordnance disposal equipment, duty gear and nuclear safety products. Our highly engineered products are utilized in over 100 countries by federal, state and local law enforcement, fire and rescue professionals, explosive ordnance disposal teams, and emergency medical technicians. Our key brands include Safariland® and Med-Eng®, amongst others.

Use of Non-GAAP Measures

​

The Company reports its financial results in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). The press release contains the non-GAAP measures: (i) earnings before interest, taxes, other income or expense, depreciation and amortization (“EBITDA”), (ii) adjusted EBITDA, (iii) adjusted EBITDA margin, (iv) last twelve months adjusted EBITDA, and (v) net debt. The Company believes the presentation of these non-GAAP measures provides useful information for the understanding of its ongoing operations and enables investors to focus on period- over-period operating performance, and thereby enhances the user’s overall understanding of the Company’s current financial performance relative to past performance and provides, along with the nearest GAAP measures, a baseline for modeling future earnings expectations. Non-GAAP measures are reconciled to comparable GAAP financial measures within this press release. We do not provide a reconciliation of the non-GAAP guidance measure adjusted EBITDA for the fiscal year 2026 to net income for the fiscal year 2026, the most comparable GAAP financial measure, due to the inherent difficulty of forecasting certain types of expenses and gains, without unreasonable effort, which affect net income but not adjusted EBITDA. The Company cautions that non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to, but not as a substitute for, the Company’s reported GAAP results. Additionally, the Company notes that there can be no assurance that the above referenced non-GAAP financial measures are comparable to similarly titled financial measures used by other publicly traded companies.
