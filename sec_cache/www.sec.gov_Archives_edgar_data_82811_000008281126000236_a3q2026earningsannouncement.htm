REGAL REXNORD REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

MILWAUKEE, WI - Regal Rexnord Corporation (NYSE: RRX)

3Q Highlights
•Daily Orders Up 6% Versus PY
•Sales Of $1,588 Million, Up 6% Versus PY
•Data Center Sales Up 307%, Or Up 169% Excluding E-Pods
•GAAP Net Loss Of $12 Million* Versus PY GAAP Net Income Of $80 Million
•Adjusted EBITDA Of $351 Million Versus PY Of $339 Million, Up $12 Million, Or 4%, Versus PY
•Adjusted EBITDA Of $337 Million Excluding IEEPA Tariff Refunds, Ahead Of Expectations
•Net Debt To Adjusted EBITDA (Including Synergies) Ended 3Q At 2.9x

CEO Aamir Paul commented on the third quarter performance, "Our third quarter execution showed steady progress. We delivered adjusted EBITDA excluding IEEPA refunds slightly ahead of our expectations. We had healthy order demand across the portfolio, with 13% orders growth excluding data centers. We were encouraged by the orders growth in commercial HVAC, up 47%, aided by new air moving solutions. Discrete automation orders were up 26%. Data center order growth continues to scale, with some specific project order timing effects in the quarter."

Providing a strategic update, Paul continued, “Four months into the role, I see opportunities to accelerate profitable growth. These encompass improving operational performance, sharpening the focus of our growth agenda, and building a stronger growth mindset in our team. To improve focus and maximize value creation at the enterprise level, we have begun a comprehensive review of our portfolio, working closely with our Board and with the support of outside advisors. Ultimately the goal is directing capital to our most attractive opportunities. While this work is ongoing, we are committed to moving with urgency when we see opportunity. Today’s announcement that we are exploring the sale of our autonomous mobile solutions business in the AMC segment reflects this approach. We plan to share further updates when we have something definitive to share.”

Paul concluded, “There is significant work ahead, but we have a strong foundation and clear opportunities to build upon. I am confident in our ability to deliver on that potential and excited about what lies ahead.”
Guidance Update

We are lowering and narrowing our 2026 GAAP EPS guidance to a range of $3.76 to $4.06*. We are raising and narrowing our 2026 Adjusted Diluted EPS guidance range to $10.60 to $10.90. Our Adjusted Diluted EPS guidance range mid-point rises to $10.75.





* Includes a non-cash charge of $119 million, or $1.78 impact to Diluted EPS, in connection with a goodwill impairment recorded in the quarter primarily related to the autonomous mobile solutions ("AMS") business in the AMC segment.



Segment Performance

Segment results for the third quarter of 2026 versus the same period of the prior year are summarized below:

•Automation & Motion Control (AMC) net sales were $500.6 million, an increase of 24.5%, or an increase of 25.2% on an organic basis. Growth was broad-based, but with particular strength in data center, including $25 million of sales related to E-Pods, and in discrete automation. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 19.2% of net sales or 18.7% excluding net IEEPA refund benefits.

•Industrial Powertrain Solutions (IPS) net sales were $649.5 million, a decrease of 1.9%, or a decrease of 1.8% on an organic basis driven by weakness in metals and mining partially offset by strength in discrete automation and energy markets. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 26.0% of net sales or 25.2% excluding net IEEPA refund benefits.

•Power Efficiency Solutions (PES) net sales were $437.9 million, an increase of 1.2%, or an increase of 0.4% on an organic basis. Growth in the commercial HVAC market was partially offset by weakness in the residential HVAC and pool markets. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 19.7% of net sales, or 18.1% excluding net IEEPA refund benefits.

About Regal Rexnord

Regal Rexnord’s 30,000 associates around the world help create a better tomorrow by providing sustainable solutions that power, transmit and control motion. The Company’s electric motors and air moving subsystems provide the power to create motion. A portfolio of highly engineered power transmission components and subsystems efficiently transmits motion to power industrial applications. The Company’s automation offering, comprised of controllers, drives, precision motors, and actuators, controls motion in applications ranging from factory automation to precision tools used in surgical applications.

The Company’s end markets benefit from meaningful secular demand tailwinds, and include discrete automation, food & beverage, aerospace & defense, medical, data center, energy, residential and commercial buildings, general industrial, and metals and mining.

Regal Rexnord is comprised of three operating segments: Automation & Motion Control, Industrial Powertrain Solutions, and Power Efficiency Solutions. Regal Rexnord is headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin and has manufacturing, sales and service facilities worldwide. For more information, including a copy of our Sustainability Report, visit RegalRexnord.com.
