Mobile Infrastructure Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

RevPAS Increases 16% Driven by Utilization Improvement

Contract Volumes Up 13% and Transient Volumes Up 11%

Conference Call to be held at 4:30 PM Eastern Time Today

 

CINCINNATI — (BUSINESSWIRE) — Mobile Infrastructure Corporation (Nasdaq: BEEP), (“Mobile”, “Mobile Infrastructure” or the “Company”), the nation’s only publicly traded owner of parking infrastructure, today reported results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

“We are pleased with our third quarter results, marking Mobile’s strongest operating quarter of 2026, building strong momentum heading into 2027, and achieving our highest utilization and RevPAS to date,” said Stephanie Hogue, Chief Executive Officer. “At the start of the year, we discussed our strategy of building utilization first, establishing a larger and more durable base of recurring and transient demand, and following this with improved, sustainable pricing as utilization improves. Our results in the third quarter evidence strong execution as our strategy translates into earnings improvement. Same-Location Revenue grew 12.8% and Same-Location NOI increased 18.4%, marking our third consecutive quarter of Same-Location NOI growth and a clear acceleration from the first half of the year.

 

“In the third quarter, our growth was principally volume-driven. Utilization rose approximately 13 percentage points year-over-year and RevPAS increased approximately 16%, reaching its highest level since we began tracking the measure in 2023. Monthly parking is the foundation of that progress. Baseline contract volumes grew approximately 13%, supported by residential demand, commercial relationships, and targeted business development, with Cincinnati, Fort Worth, and Detroit among the leading contributors. Recurring contracts give us greater revenue visibility and a more stable base from which to optimize transient inventory, which supports improved pricing over time. Transient demand benefited from an active summer event calendar and a growing share of third-party reservations, with notable strength in Chicago, Cincinnati, and Nashville. We have deliberately prioritized utilization and customer relationships, and we expect to achieve pricing power as more of the portfolio reaches stabilized utilization.

 

“We continue to execute our disciplined capital allocation strategy. To date, we have generated approximately $33 million of proceeds under our three-year $100 million asset rotation program, at a weighted-average capitalization rate of approximately 2%. These private-market valuations reinforce the underlying value of our portfolio and the disconnect from our current share price. Our priorities remain clear: maximize the performance of our existing assets, improve portfolio quality and strengthen the balance sheet, all to build long-term shareholder value,” Ms. Hogue concluded.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

  ● Total revenue was $9.6 million, up 5.5%, as compared to $9.1 million in the prior-year period and $8.9 million in Q2.
  ● Same-Location Revenue was $9.6 million, up 12.8%, as compared to $8.5 million in the prior-year period.
  ● Net loss was $2.6 million as compared to $6.4 million in the prior-year period and $3.2 million in Q2.
  ● NOI* was $6.3 million as compared to $5.5 million in the prior-year period, an increase of 13.6% year-over-year.
  ● Same-Location NOI* was $6.3 million as compared to $5.3 million in the prior-year period, an increase of 18.4% year-over-year.
  ● Adjusted EBITDA* was $4.8 million as compared to $3.9 million in the prior-year period, an increase of 23.2% year-over-year.
  ● Contract parking volumes grew approximately 13% year-over-year, supported by continued strength in residential and return-to-office momentum.

 

* Explanations of these non-GAAP financial measures and reconciliation to the most comparable GAAP financial measures are presented later in this press release.

 

Q3 2026 Financial Results

 

Total revenue of $9.6 million increased by 5.5% from $9.1 million in the prior-year quarter. Same-Location Revenue was $9.6 million, an increase of 12.8% compared to $8.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue increases are primarily due to growth in managed property revenue driven by contract and transient volume increases.

 

Total property taxes and operating expenses were $3.3 million, as compared to $3.6 million during the third quarter of 2025.

General and administrative expenses were $2.3 million, which included $0.8 million of non-cash compensation, compared to $2.4 million during the third quarter of 2025, which included $0.8 million of non-cash compensation.

 

Interest expense was $4.8 million compared to $4.6 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Net loss was $2.6 million, an improvement from $6.4 million in the prior-year period, which included a non-cash $2.5 million impairment of real estate.

 

Same-Location Net Operating Income (“Same-Location NOI”), defined by the Company as total revenues less property taxes and operating expenses for the 35 properties owned during both reported periods, was $6.3 million, up 18.4% from $5.3 million in the prior-year period. Growth was driven primarily by contract and transient volume increases together with disciplined operating expense control. Adjusted EBITDA was $4.8 million compared to $3.9 million in the prior-year period.

 

Revenue Per Available Stall (“RevPAS”) was $245.83, compared to $211.53 in the third quarter of 2025 and $224.96 in the second quarter of 2026. Trailing twelve-month RevPAS was $211.22, compared to $202.53 a year earlier. Portfolio utilization was up approximately 13 percentage points year-over-year in the third quarter and approximately 8 percentage points on a trailing twelve-month basis, reflecting continued contract growth and the reopening of several demand-driving venues across the portfolio driving transient volumes.

 

Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Liquidity

 

At September 30, 2026, the Company had $10.6 million in cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash, compared to $15.3 million at December 31, 2025. As of September 30, 2026, total debt outstanding, net, including outstanding borrowings under the Line of Credit and notes payable, was $198.3 million, down from $207.7 million at yearend 2025.

 

Full Year 2026 Guidance**

 

The Company is reiterating its full year 2026 guidance range as initially provided with fourth quarter and full year 2025 results. Based on our results to date, for full year 2026, the Company expects revenue to be at the low end of the range of $35 million to $38 million.

 

The Company expects NOI to range from $21.5 million to $23.0 million, representing year-over-year growth of 7% at the midpoint, and 10% growth on a same-location basis. The Company expects adjusted EBITDA to range from $15.0 million to $16.5 million, representing year-over-year growth of 10% at the midpoint, and 13% growth on a same-location basis.

 

This guidance is supported by expectations for continued contract volume growth, the reopening and enhancement of several venues, and the positive impact from technology optimization across the Company’s core portfolio on pricing and utilization. The guidance does not reflect any potential future asset sales or acquisitions from the asset rotation plan.

Discussion and Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures

 

Same-Location Net Operating Income

 

Net Operating Income (“NOI”) is presented as a supplemental measure of our performance. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, Same-Location NOI represents the NOI for the 35 properties that were owned for both calendar year periods being compared. The Company believes that NOI provides useful information to investors regarding our results of operations, as it highlights operating trends such as pricing and demand for our portfolio at the property level as opposed to the corporate level. NOI is calculated as total revenues less property operating expenses and property taxes. The Company uses NOI internally in evaluating property performance, measuring property operating trends, and valuing properties in our portfolio. Other real estate companies may use different methodologies for calculating NOI, and accordingly, the Company’s NOI may not be comparable to other real estate companies. NOI should not be viewed as an alternative measure of financial performance as it does not reflect the impact of general and administrative expenses, depreciation and amortization, interest expense, other income and expenses, or the level of capital expenditures necessary to maintain the operating performance of the Company’s properties that could materially impact results from operations.

 

Adjusted EBITDA

 

Adjusted Earnings Before Interest Expense, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”) reflects net income (loss) excluding the impact of interest expense, depreciation and amortization, and the provision for income taxes, for all periods presented. Adjusted EBITDA also excludes certain recurring and non-recurring items including, but not limited to, stock-based compensation expense, non-cash changes in fair value of the Earn-Out Liability, gains or losses from disposition of real estate assets, impairment write-downs of depreciable property, and Other Income, Net. Adjusted EBITDA should be considered along with, but not as an alternative to, net income (loss), cash flow from operations or any other operating GAAP measure.

RevPAS

 

Revenue Per Available Stall (“RevPAS”) is used to evaluate parking operations and performance. RevPAS is defined as average monthly Parking Revenue (Parking Revenue less related Sales Tax and Credit Card Fees) divided by the parking stalls in the locations that were owned and under management agreement for the periods presented. Parking Revenue does not include Billboard or Commercial Rent, or revenue from locations that are under Lease Agreements. The Company believes RevPAS is a meaningful indicator of our performance because it measures the period-over-period change in revenues for comparable locations.
