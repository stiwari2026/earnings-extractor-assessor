Solaris Energy Infrastructure Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results, Additional Power Contracts and Increased Guidance

HOUSTON, Texas, November 4, 2026 — (BUSINESS WIRE) — Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc. (NYSE:SEI) (“Solaris” or the “Company”), today announced the following:
Third Quarter 2026 Summary Results
◦Revenue of approximately $341 million increased 55% sequentially from second quarter 2026.
◦Net income of $39 million, which increased 56% from second quarter 2026, and $0.42 per diluted Class A common share; Adjusted pro forma net income(1) of $43 million, which increased 18% from second quarter 2026, and $0.45 per fully diluted share.
◦Adjusted EBITDA(1) of approximately $136 million increased 26% sequentially from second quarter 2026.
◦Adjusted EBITDA attributable to Solaris(1)(4) of approximately $138 million, which excludes the EBITDA impact attributable to the non-controlling interest in Stateline Power, LLC (“Stateline”), the Company’s joint venture.
Recent Growth Initiatives
◦New 15-Year Power Contract Signed. On October 29, 2026, the Company entered into new 15-year agreement, with an option to extend for an additional 15-years, to provide approximately 900 MW of power generation, associated balance of plant equipment, and operations and maintenance services. The agreement is with a leading, investment-grade, diversified industrial and technology company that is an existing customer of the Company. Deployments under this agreement are expected to begin in 1H 2027 and scale through 2028.
▪Represents the Third, Long-term Power Contract Signed with Investment Grade counterparties year to date, bringing total capacity under long-term contracts to 3.2 GW.
◦Secured an Additional 200 MW of Available Generation Capacity. Procured time-advantaged equipment that will be deployed under contracts with customers in 2027, ahead of long OEM queues. Pro forma consolidated generation capacity is now approximately 3.5 GW in 2029.
◦New Operating & Maintenance Contract Signed. The Company signed a new 7-year operating and maintenance agreement to manage approximately 400 MW of generation capacity owned by an existing customer.
◦Strengthened Balance Sheet with $1.5 billion of Growth Financing. Successfully completed a $1.25 billion senior, unsecured notes offering and secured additional $200 million of bank commitments, bringing total revolving credit facility capacity to $850 million (currently undrawn). Corporate credit ratings were reaffirmed by S&P Global Ratings (BB-), Moody’s Ratings (Ba3) and Fitch Ratings (BB).
Increasing Guidance (1)(2)
◦Increasing fourth quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $165-185 million from $145-180 million and increasing first quarter 2027 Adjusted EBITDA guidance to $220-260 million from $200-240 million.
Shareholder Returns
◦On October 27, 2026, the Company’s board of directors approved a fourth quarter 2026 dividend of $0.12 per share, to be paid on December 18, 2026, to holders of record as of December 8, 2026, which, once paid, will represent Solaris’ 33rd consecutive dividend.
CEO Commentary
“Solaris’ transformation into a comprehensive power infrastructure solutions provider is driving record results in the near term and positioning Solaris for significant long-term value creation. Our quarterly Adjusted EBITDA has more than doubled over the last year, and we expect 2027 Adjusted EBITDA to be more than double 2026, with further growth beyond that,” Bill Zartler, Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer, and Amanda Brock, Co-Chief Executive Officer,

Solaris Power Solutions
•Activity – Third quarter 2026 averaged approximately 820 MW of capacity earning revenue, which decreased 14% compared to approximately 950 MW in second quarter 2026 primarily due to the planned release of temporary third-party generation as we transition to owned capacity under long-term contracts.

•Profitability – Third quarter 2026 Segment Adjusted EBITDA (1)(3) of approximately $127 million increased 32% from second quarter 2026 due primarily to increased services revenue.

•Revenue – Third quarter 2026 revenue of $57 million decreased 7% from second quarter 2026 due to lower last-mile transportation activity.
•Profitability – Third quarter 2026 Segment Adjusted EBITDA (1)(3) of $24 million decreased 3% from second quarter 2026 in line with activity.

About Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc.
Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc. (NYSE:SEI) and its consolidated subsidiaries deliver comprehensive power infrastructure solutions including generation, distribution, site preparation, installation and commissioning, aftermarket support, and operations and maintenance. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, the Company serves multiple end markets, including data centers, energy, and other commercial and industrial sectors. EBITDA AND ADJUSTED EBITDA
We view EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA as important indicators of performance. We use them to assess our results of operations because it allows us, our investors and our lenders to compare our operating performance on a consistent basis across periods by removing the effects of varying levels of interest expense due to our capital structure, depreciation and amortization due to our asset base and other items that impact the comparability of financial results from period to period. We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because we believe they provide useful information regarding trends and other factors affecting our business in addition to measures calculated under generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”).
We define EBITDA as net income, plus (i) depreciation and amortization expense, (ii) interest (income) expense and (iii) income tax expense. We define Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA plus (i) stock-based compensation expense and (ii) certain non-cash items and extraordinary, unusual or non-recurring gains, losses or expenses.
EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for an analysis of our results of operation and financial condition as reported in accordance with GAAP. Net income is the GAAP measure most directly comparable to EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered alternatives to net income presented in accordance with GAAP. Because EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA may be defined differently by other companies in our industry, our definitions of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies, thereby diminishing their utility.

ADJUSTED PRO FORMA NET INCOME AND ADJUSTED PRO FORMA EARNINGS PER FULLY DILUTED SHARE
Adjusted pro forma net income represents net income attributable to Solaris assuming the full exchange of all outstanding membership interests in Solaris Energy Infrastructure, LLC (“Solaris LLC”) not held by Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc. for shares of Class A common stock, adjusted for certain non-recurring items that the Company does not believe directly reflect its core operations and may not be indicative of ongoing business operations. Adjusted pro forma earnings per fully diluted share is calculated by dividing adjusted pro forma net income by the weighted-average shares of Class A common stock outstanding, assuming the full exchange of all outstanding units of Solaris LLC (“Solaris LLC Units”), after giving effect to the dilutive effect of outstanding equity-based awards.
When used in conjunction with GAAP financial measures, adjusted pro forma net income and adjusted pro forma earnings per fully diluted share are supplemental measures of operating performance that the Company believes are useful measures to evaluate performance period over period and relative to its competitors. By assuming the full exchange of all outstanding Solaris LLC Units, the Company believes these measures facilitate comparisons with other companies that have different organizational and tax structures, as well as comparisons period over period because it eliminates the effect of any changes in net income attributable to Solaris as a result of increases in its ownership of Solaris LLC, which are unrelated to the Company's operating performance, and excludes items that are non-recurring or may not be indicative of ongoing operating performance.
Adjusted pro forma net income and adjusted pro forma earnings per fully diluted share are not necessarily comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies due to different methods of calculation. Presentation of adjusted pro forma net income and adjusted pro forma earnings per fully diluted share should not be considered alternatives to net income and earnings per share, as determined under GAAP. While these measures are useful in evaluating the Company's performance, they do not account for the earnings attributable to the non-controlling interest holders and therefore do not provide a complete understanding of the net income attributable to Solaris. Adjusted pro forma net income and adjusted pro forma earnings per fully diluted share should be evaluated in conjunction with GAAP financial results.
