Intelligent Bio Solutions Reports Record Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Revenue of $1.39 Million, Marking Sixth Consecutive Quarter of Year-Over-Year Growth

 

Revenue up 25% year-over-year and 21% sequentially, marking third consecutive quarter above $1 million

 

Higher-margin cartridge sales grew 32% year-over-year and 35% sequentially, representing 61% of total revenue

 

Gross profit margin expanded to 54.5%, up from 46.6% in the prior-year quarter

 

Company submitted its FDA 510(k) premarket notification during the quarter, a key step toward planned U.S. market entry

 

NEW YORK, November 10, 2026 – Intelligent Bio Solutions Inc. (Nasdaq: INBS) (“INBS” or the “Company”), a medical technology company delivering intelligent, rapid, non-invasive testing solutions, today announced its financial results and operational highlights for the fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Fiscal first quarter revenue was the highest quarterly revenue in our Company’s history,” said Harry Simeonidis, President and CEO at Intelligent Bio Solutions. “Each reader draws on a recurring supply of higher-margin cartridges, which is driving most of our revenue growth. Our business continues to scale with improving unit economics, including expanding gross profit margins driven by manufacturing efficiencies and a higher share of revenue from higher-margin cartridge consumables.”

 

Mr. Simeonidis continued, “In September, we submitted our 510(k) premarket notification to the FDA for the Intelligent Fingerprinting Drug Screening System, the culmination of the clinical program we executed throughout 2026 and a key step toward our planned entry into the multi-billion-dollar U.S. drug screening market. Going forward, we will continue to invest in the clinical and regulatory work behind our 510(k) submission while maintaining discipline across the rest of the business.”

 

Product Revenue Breakdown

 

Three Months Ended September 30, 2026 (Q1 Fiscal 2027):

 

●Total revenue of $1.39 million, up 25% year-over-year and 21% sequentially
●Cartridge sales of $845,583, up 32% year-over-year and 35% sequentially, representing 61% of Q1 revenue

●Cash and cash equivalents of $5.93 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $3.99 million at June 30, 2026

 

Fiscal First Quarter 2027 and Subsequent Highlights

 

●Record quarterly revenue: Revenue of $1,391,835 surpassed the Company’s prior quarterly record of $1,145,802, set in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, and marked the third consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth and the third consecutive quarter above $1 million.
●Cartridge consumables driving growth: Cartridge sales accounted for 73% of the $280,038 year-over-year increase in total revenue and grew to 61% of quarterly revenue, up from 58% a year ago.
●Gross profit margin above 54%: Gross profit margin of 54.5% improved approximately 790 basis points year-over-year, reflecting manufacturing efficiencies and a growing share of higher-margin cartridge sales.
●FDA 510(k) submitted: On September 9, 2026, the Company submitted its 510(k) premarket notification to the FDA, seeking clearance of the Intelligent Fingerprinting Drug Screening System for detection of the opiate codeine. The submission included a method comparison study that demonstrated accuracy above 98%, sensitivity above 94%, and specificity of 100% across three independent U.S. clinical sites.
●510(k) clinical program completed: During the quarter, the Company completed precision testing, interference, usability, and method comparison studies, and resolved all cybersecurity review items, finalizing the body of clinical evidence supporting its submission.
●ISO 13485:2016 recertification: The Company’s Intelligent Fingerprinting Drug Screening business was recertified to the ISO 13485:2016 quality management system standard by the British Standards Institution, supporting alignment with the FDA’s amended Quality Management System Regulation.
●Customer and installed base growth: 516 active customer accounts as of September 30, 2026, including 27 new accounts added during the quarter, with a reader installed base of 1,978 units.

 

The fiscal first quarter extended INBS’s consistent run of growth, with the installed base built through prior reader placements continuing to drive recurring, higher-margin cartridge demand. With its 510(k) submission complete, record quarterly revenue, and expanding gross profit margins, management believes the Company is well-positioned to continue its growth through fiscal 2027 as it pursues its planned entry into the multi-billion-dollar U.S. drug screening market.

About Intelligent Bio Solutions Inc. 

 

Intelligent Bio Solutions Inc. (Nasdaq: INBS) is a medical technology company delivering intelligent, rapid, non-invasive testing solutions. The Company believes that its Intelligent Fingerprinting Drug Testing Solution, comprising its portable, on-site Intelligent Fingerprinting Drug Screening System (“System”) and its Confirmatory Analysis Kit for back-to-lab testing, will revolutionize portable testing through fingerprint sweat analysis, which has the potential for broader applications in additional fields. Designed as a fast, hygienic, and cost-effective solution, the System screens for the recent use of common drugs of abuse, including opiates, cocaine, methamphetamine, and cannabis. With sample collection in seconds and results in under ten minutes, this technology would be a valuable tool across workplace, law enforcement, and government settings. The Company’s current customer segments outside the U.S. include construction and engineering, manufacturing, transport and logistics, mining, energy and utilities, government and public services, law enforcement and forensics, and drug treatment and rehabilitation.

 

Intelligent Bio Solutions Inc. is an authorized distributor of the SMARTOX SmarTest Patch. The SmarTest Patch is a discreet, water-resistant, and tamper-evident device worn on the skin for 7 to 10 days at a time, continuously collecting sweat to detect a broad range of substances, including cocaine, opiates, methamphetamine, marijuana, and fentanyl. The differing detection windows of the SmarTest Patch and the System allow the Company to address both short- and long-term detection needs while maintaining a consistent, non-invasive, sweat-based approach.
