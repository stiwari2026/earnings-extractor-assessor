“We delivered another quarter of outstanding performance, highlighted by nearly 14% revenue growth, increasing margins, strong cash flow, and robust earnings per share growth. Our results reflected sustained positive momentum across our revenue growth algorithm, with continued excellent base growth, solid new logo contribution, successful upsell and cross-sell activity, and consistently high retention. We continue to see broad-based growth across our diverse, balanced verticals, with strong performance from nearly every vertical in our Americas business. Our AI-enabled products continue to be key differentiators as we help our customers mitigate human capital risks and prevent fraud. Our go-to-market momentum and pipeline remain strong as we approach 2027,” said Scott Staples, Chief Executive Officer.

“On October 31, we celebrated the two-year anniversary of closing the Sterling acquisition. We are executing well and realizing benefits far exceeding our original expectations. Today, we are a larger, stronger, and more diversified company, offering customers a broader portfolio of best-of-breed technologies and products backed by greater scale and innovation capabilities. As a result, we have strengthened our market position, expanded our growth opportunities, and enhanced our ability to create long-term value for shareholders,” Staples added.

Acquisition of Data Diver Technologies

“In addition, today we are excited to announce our acquisition of Data Diver Technologies, a data platform leveraging AI and automation that collects, aggregates, retains, and delivers high-volume criminal and court records, further supporting our strategy of delivering fast, accurate, and automated screening results for our customers. This acquisition aligns squarely with our focus on investing in data and technology assets to help our customers make decisions faster and mitigate risk, while enhancing our proprietary data and automation capabilities and creating new opportunities to expand our solutions and drive product innovation. This acquisition represents another important step in advancing our FA 5.0 growth strategy,” Staples concluded.

The related press release is available on First Advantage’s investor relations website.

Raising Full Year 2026 Guidance

“This quarter, we continued to progress on our synergy program for the Sterling acquisition and expect to finish the year near or at the top end of our run rate synergy target range, underscoring the ongoing success of our integration efforts. Looking forward, our focus is on driving continued operational efficiencies, supporting product innovation, and translating the benefits of the combination into sustained growth, margin expansion, and cash flow generation. While deleveraging remains a high priority for capital allocation, as evidenced by our $45 million debt prepayment during the quarter, our consistent cash flow provides us flexibility for disciplined, strategic capital deployment, including the Data Diver Technologies acquisition, to complement our organic growth objectives and drive incremental shareholder returns,” said Steven Marks, Chief Financial Officer. “We remain confident in our ability to deliver consistent profitable growth. Based on our strong performance through the first three quarters of the year and our expectations for the fourth quarter, we are raising our guidance ranges for the full year, with the low end of our updated guidance ranges now exceeding the high end of our prior guidance ranges across every metric.”

Non-GAAP Financial Information

This press release contains “non-GAAP financial measures” that are financial measures that either exclude or include amounts that are not excluded or included in the most directly comparable measures calculated and presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). Specifically, we make use of the non-GAAP financial measures “Adjusted EBITDA,” “Adjusted EBITDA Margin,” “Adjusted Net Income,” and “Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share.”

Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share have been presented in this press release as supplemental measures of financial performance that are not required by or presented in accordance with GAAP because we believe they assist investors and analysts in comparing our operating performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance. Management believes these non-GAAP measures are useful to investors in highlighting trends in our operating performance, while other measures can differ significantly depending on long-term strategic decisions regarding capital structure, the tax jurisdictions in which we operate, and capital investments. Management uses Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share to supplement GAAP measures of performance in the evaluation of the effectiveness of our business strategies, to make budgeting decisions, to establish discretionary annual incentive compensation, and to compare our performance against that of peer companies using similar measures. Management supplements GAAP results with non-GAAP financial measures to provide a more complete understanding of the factors and trends affecting the business than GAAP results alone.

Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share are not recognized terms under GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to net income as a measure of financial performance or cash provided by operating activities as a measure of liquidity, or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP.

We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, and as further adjusted for loss on extinguishment of debt, share-based compensation, transaction and acquisition-related charges, integration and restructuring charges, and other non-cash charges. We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by total revenues. We define Adjusted Net Income for a particular period as net income before taxes adjusted for debt-related costs, acquisition-related depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation, transaction and acquisition-related charges, integration and restructuring charges, and other non-cash charges, to which we then apply the related effective tax rate. We define Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share as Adjusted Net Income divided by adjusted weighted average number of shares outstanding—diluted.

For reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures, see the reconciliations included at the end of this press release.

The presentations of these measures have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Because not all companies use identical calculations, the presentations of these measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies and can differ significantly from company to company.

About First Advantage

First Advantage (NASDAQ: FA) is a global software and data company. We provide comprehensive, end-to-end identity solutions, criminal background screening, credential verifications, drug and health screening, and continuous risk monitoring. Combining AI-powered proprietary technology platforms with proprietary data, primary source data, and third-party data, we help organizations hire with confidence and manage risk across the entire employee lifecycle. With over 80,000 customers worldwide – including approximately two-thirds of the Fortune 100 – we deliver fast, comprehensive, and reliable solutions for employers, their candidates, and their employees. We conduct more than 200 million screens annually across over 200 countries and territories, supported by our verticalized go-to-market strategy, decades of experience, and proprietary databases containing over 1 billion records. For more information, please visit our website at https://fadv.com/.
