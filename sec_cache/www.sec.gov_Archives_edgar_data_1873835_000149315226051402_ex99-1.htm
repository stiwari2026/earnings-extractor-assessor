Immix Biopharma Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Update on Corporate Progress

 

– Following positive NEXICART-2 clinical update, commercial launch preparation continues with commercial site activation activities, strengthened team, and provider and payer engagement –

 

– Interim NEXICART-2 clinical trial results selected for oral presentation at ASH 2026 –

 

LOS ANGELES, November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Immix Biopharma, Inc. (“Immix”, the “Company”, “we” or “us”), a global leader in AL amyloidosis, today reported an update on corporate progress and financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Recent NXC-201 Progress

 

-Positive clinical data: 89% complete response rate was reported across all 45 NEXICART-2 patients, with MRD-negativity indicating potential to reach up to 98% complete response rate in the future.
-Immix continues to advance its AL Amyloidosis launch readiness activities across commercial site activation, provider and payer engagement, manufacturing activities, and healthcare professional education.

 

Immix plans for its BLA submission in mid-2027, and NEXICART-3 front line trial initiation in the first half of 2027.

 

Interim NXC-201 NEXICART-2 Clinical Trial Results Selected for Oral Presentation at 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting

 

On Monday, December 14, 2026, NXC-201 NEXICART-2 interim clinical data will be presented at the 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting in New Orleans, LA.

 

Commercial Launch Preparation Continues with Strengthened Team

 

Immix has appointed David He, MD, PhD as Senior Vice President, Head of Technical Operations. Dr. He has contributed in a senior role to two approved BCMA CAR-Ts, CARVYKTI® and FUCASO®. Dr. He will lead chemistry, manufacturing, and controls at Immix, further accelerating the Company’s planned path to commercialization. Prior to Immix, Dr. He served as the Chief Technology Officer from pre-BLA to commercial-stage at IASO Biotherapeutics and as the Vice President, Technical Operations at Legend Biotech, as well as in a senior chemistry, manufacturing, and controls positions at Boehringer Ingelheim, and other senior roles.

 

Immix has appointed Karin McIntosh as Senior Vice President, Head of Regulatory Affairs. Karin has contributed to multiple FDA drug programs, including JANUVIA® (FDA+EMA), EMFLAZA® (FDA), ELZONRIS® (FDA+EMA), and most recently, the approval of TREGZI® (FDA, 2026). Karin will lead regulatory affairs at Immix in the United States and globally. Prior to Immix, Karin was Vice President, Head of Regulatory Affairs at Orca Bio and held senior regulatory positions at Stemline Therapeutics, PTC Therapeutics, Merck, and Pfizer.

About AL Amyloidosis

 

Amyloid light-chain (AL) amyloidosis is a devastating disease where the immune system, that’s supposed to protect, instead continuously produces toxic light chains that build up in organs including the heart, kidney, and liver, causing organ failure and death.

 

AL amyloidosis is the most common form of amyloidosis according to the National Institutes of Health. The number of patients in the U.S. with relapsed/refractory AL Amyloidosis is estimated to be growing at 12% per year according to Staron, et al Blood Cancer Journal, to approximately 30,506 patients in 2026.

 

The Amyloidosis market was $6.2 billion in 2025, and is expected to reach $6.6 billion in 2026, according to Grand View Research.

 

About NXC-201

 

NXC-201 is a sterically-optimized BCMA-targeted chimeric antigen receptor T (CAR-T) cell therapy with a proprietary CD3ζ, CD8 hinge and binder “digital filter” designed to filter out non-specific activation. NXC-201 teaches the immune system to recognize and eliminate the source of the toxic light chains. NXC-201 has been awarded Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD) and Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) by the FDA, and Orphan Drug Designation (ODD) by the US FDA and in the EU by the EMA.

About Immix Biopharma, Inc.

 

Immix Biopharma, Inc. (Nasdaq: IMMX) is a global leader in AL amyloidosis. AL amyloidosis is a devastating disease where the immune system, that’s supposed to protect, instead produces toxic light chains, clogging up the heart, kidney and liver, causing organ failure and death. Our lead candidate is sterically-optimized BCMA-targeted chimeric antigen receptor T (CAR-T) cell therapy NXC-201 with a proprietary CD3ζ, CD8 hinge and binder “digital filter” designed to filter out non-specific activation. NXC-201 teaches the immune system to recognize and eliminate the source of the toxic light chains. NXC-201 is being evaluated in the U.S. multi-center study for relapsed/refractory AL amyloidosis NEXICART-2 (NCT06097832), with a potentially registrational design. NXC-201 has been awarded Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD) and Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) by the US FDA and Orphan Drug Designation (ODD) by FDA and in the EU by the EMA.
