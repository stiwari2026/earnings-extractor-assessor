IMAX CORPORATION REPORTS RECORD THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

•IMAX delivers best quarterly financial performance in company history, breaking records across key metrics including:

◦Revenue of $175 million, up 64% year-over-year and Gross Margin of $126 million, up 88% year-over-year

◦Strong operating leverage driven profitability with Net Income(1) of $117 million at a record margin of 67% and Adjusted EBITDA(2) of $121 million at a record margin of 69%, up over 2000 basis points year-over-year

◦Net income per diluted share of $1.96, up 430% year-over year and Adjusted EPS(1,2) of $2.21, up 370% year-over-year - both quarterly figures eclipsing any full year result in IMAX history

◦Cash from Operating Activities of $86 million, up 28% year-over-year

•Company reports record quarterly global box office of $712 million — up +90% over the previous quarterly record of $372 million.

•Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey makes history as highest grossing IMAX release of all time, with over $550 million in worldwide box office and delivering more than 30% of the film’s global box office

•Signings for IMAX systems reach over 100 year-to-date — 65% new locations — as sales activity accelerates following the record-breaking performance of The Odyssey and amidst strong pre-sales for December’s Dune: Part Three

•Dune: Part Three and Chinese release of Avengers: Doomsday kick off promising 2027 slate currently taking shape, with strong Chinese New Year offering led by Wandering Earth 3; Filmed for IMAX releases including Star Wars: Starfighter and Superman sequel Man of Tomorrow; Avengers: Secret Wars; and the IMAX debut of the original Star Wars, presented in IMAX 70mm film for the first time ever

NEW YORK, NY — November 5, 2026 — IMAX Corporation (NYSE: IMAX) today reported the best quarterly financial results in company history for the third quarter of 2026, as the global entertainment platform surged to new heights with record global box office in the period.

“What IMAX achieved goes beyond simply a ‘great quarter’ and transcends the many records we shattered in the period. What we believe we are witnessing is an awakening to the power and potential of our platform to impact global culture, unlock new commercial opportunities, and inspire a new level of demand and awe in audiences around the world.,” said Rich Gelfond, CEO of IMAX Corporation.”

“Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey is a historic cinematic achievement crafted entirely with IMAX technology and its record-breaking success marks a new era of opportunity for our company. The record margins we generated in the quarter demonstrate the unique strength of our model, and the growing value of scarce events and experiences in an increasingly A.I.-driven world underscore a powerful opportunity for our business, as we look ahead to a remarkably strong slate over the next several years.”

“Our momentum is fueling sales activity with exhibitors around the world, with our team delivering more than 100 signings for IMAX systems worldwide year-to-date — including 65% new locations and strong growth across priority Rest of World markets.”

“With the global release of Dune: Part Three and our release of Avengers: Doomsday in China carrying into a very strong 2027 slate, we are excited for the year ahead and confident in our belief that there has never been a better time to be in the IMAX business. This ‘IMAX awakening’ sits inside a bigger culture shift of people around the world — particularly young people — seeking connection, community, and shared experiences that feel bigger than any one person’s screen. IMAX has always delivered these moments, and we believe this trend has only begun to benefit our business.”

Content Solutions Segment

•Third quarter Content Solutions revenues and gross margin increased 110% to $94 million and 125% to $72 million year-over-year, respectively. IMAX’s global platform delivered a record 76% gross margin reflecting the significant incrementality within the IMAX business model as our platform scales and box office increases.

•Third quarter global box office of $712 million marked IMAX’s highest quarterly box office in its history and increased 94% year-over-year leading IMAX to deliver 6.7% of the industry’s third quarter global box office (on less than 1% of worldwide screens). Top grossing third quarter titles included The Odyssey ($538 million) and Spider-Man: Brand New Day ($58 million).

Technology Products and Services Segment

•Third quarter Technology Products and Services revenues and gross margin increased 28% to $77 million and 53% to $53 million year-over-year, respectively, driven by rental revenue growth tied to higher box office and amendments and renewals of existing agreements which more than offset lower systems under sales arrangements installations.

•During the third quarter of 2026, the Company installed 32 systems compared to 38 systems in the third quarter of 2025. Of the quarter’s installs, 15 systems were under sales arrangements, compared to 17 in the prior year.

•Commercial network growth continued with the number of IMAX locations reaching 1,820 systems as of September 30, 2026 compared to 1,759 systems as of September 30, 2025. The Company ended Q3 2026 with a backlog of 409 IMAX systems.

Operating Cash Flow and Liquidity

Net cash provided by operating activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased 25% year-over-year to $122 million, reflecting higher operating profits and the elevated conversion of profits to cash flow as margins increase.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s available liquidity was $641 million. The Company’s liquidity included cash and cash equivalents of $208 million, $375 million in available borrowing capacity under the Company’s revolving credit facility, and $58 million in available borrowing capacity under IMAX China’s revolving facilities. Total debt, excluding deferred financing costs, was $251 million as of September 30, 2026.

In 2025, the Company issued $250 million of 0.750% Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 (“2030 Convertible Notes”). In connection with the pricing of the 2030 Convertible Notes, the Company entered into privately negotiated capped call transactions with a cap price of $57.10 per share of the Company’s common shares.

About IMAX Corporation

IMAX, an innovator in entertainment technology, combines proprietary software, architecture, and equipment to create experiences that take you beyond the edge of your seat to a world you’ve never imagined. Top filmmakers and studios are utilizing IMAX systems to connect with audiences in extraordinary ways, making IMAX’s network among the most important and successful theatrical distribution platforms for major event films around the globe.

IMAX’s principal executive offices are located in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada and New York, New York. As of September 30, 2026, there were 1,887 IMAX systems (1,820 commercial multiplexes, 10 commercial destinations, 57 institutional locations) operating in 91 countries and territories.

Primary Reporting Groups

The Company’s Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”) is its Chief Operating Decision Maker (“CODM”), as such term is defined under U.S. GAAP. The CODM assesses segment performance based on segment revenues and segment gross margins. Selling, general and administrative expenses, research and development costs, the amortization of intangible assets, provision for (reversal of) current expected credit losses, certain write-downs, interest income, interest expense, and income tax (expense) benefit are not allocated to the Company’s segments.

The Company has two reportable segments:

(i)Content Solutions, which consists of services provided to studios and other content creators, and principally includes the digital remastering of films and other content into IMAX formats for distribution to the IMAX network. To a lesser extent, the Content Solutions segment also earns revenue from the distribution of large-format documentary films and exclusive experiences ranging from live performances to interactive events with leading artists and creators, as well as film post-production services.

(ii)Technology Products and Services, which includes results from the sale or lease of IMAX Systems, as well as from the maintenance of IMAX Systems to exhibition customers. To a lesser extent, the Technology Product and Services segment also earns revenue from certain ancillary theater business activities, including after-market sales of IMAX System parts and 3D glasses.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

In this release, the Company presents adjusted net income attributable to common shareholders and adjusted net income attributable to common shareholders per diluted share, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA per Credit Facility, Adjusted EBITDA margin and free cash flow as supplemental measures of the Company’s performance, which are not recognized under U.S. GAAP.

A reconciliation from net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders and net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders per diluted share to adjusted net income attributable to common shareholders and adjusted net income attributable to common shareholders per diluted share is presented in the table below. Net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders and net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders per diluted share are the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures because they reflect the earnings relevant to the Company’s shareholders, rather than the earnings attributable to non-controlling interests.

Adjusted net income or loss attributable to common shareholders and adjusted net income or loss attributable to common shareholders per basic and diluted share exclude, where applicable: (i) share-based compensation; (ii) realized and unrealized investment gains or losses; (iii) goodwill impairment; (iv) restructuring charges and other impairments; and (v) employee retention credits, as well as the related tax impact of these adjustments.

The Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures are important supplemental measures that allow management and users of the Company’s financial statements to view operating trends and analyze controllable operating performance on a comparable basis between periods without the after-tax impact of share-based compensation and certain unusual items included in net income attributable to common shareholders. Although share-based compensation is an important aspect of the Company’s employee and executive compensation packages, it is a non-cash expense and is excluded from certain internal business performance measures.

In addition to the non-GAAP financial measures discussed above, management also uses “EBITDA,” as such term is defined in the Credit Agreement, and which is referred to herein as “Adjusted EBITDA per Credit Facility” as well as “Adjusted EBITDA margin.” As defined in the Credit Agreement, Adjusted EBITDA per Credit Facility includes adjustments in addition to the exclusion of interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. Accordingly, this non-GAAP financial measure is presented to allow a more comprehensive analysis of the Company’s operating performance and to provide additional information with respect to the Company’s compliance with its Credit Agreement requirements, when applicable. In addition, the Company believes that Adjusted EBITDA per Credit Facility and Adjusted EBITDA margin present relevant and useful information widely used by analysts, investors and other interested parties in the Company’s industry to evaluate, assess and benchmark the Company’s results.

EBITDA is defined as net income or loss excluding: (i) income tax expense or benefit; (ii) interest expense, net of interest income; (iii) depreciation and amortization, including film asset amortization; and (iv) amortization of deferred financing costs. Total Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA excluding: (i) share-based and other non-cash compensation expense; (ii) realized and unrealized investment losses or gains; (iii) restructuring charges and other impairments; (iv) write-downs including goodwill, asset impairments and credit loss expense or reversal and (v) induced conversion expense on settlement of convertible notes. Adjusted EBITDA per Credit Facility is defined as EBITDA excluding: (i) share-based and other non-cash compensation; (ii) realized and unrealized investment gains or losses; (iii) restructuring charges and other impairments; (iv) write-downs, net of recoveries, including goodwill, asset impairments and credit loss expense or reversal and (v) induced conversion expense on settlement of convertible notes. Total Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as Total Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.

In this release, the Company also presents free cash flow, which is not recognized under U.S. GAAP, as a supplemental measure of the Company’s liquidity. The Company’s definition of free cash flow deducts only normal recurring capital expenditures, including the Company’s investment in joint revenue sharing arrangements, the purchase of property, plant and equipment and the acquisition of other intangible assets (from the Condensed Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows), from net cash provided by or used in operating activities. Management believes that free cash flow is a supplemental measure of the cash flow available to reduce debt, add to cash balances, and fund other financing activities.
