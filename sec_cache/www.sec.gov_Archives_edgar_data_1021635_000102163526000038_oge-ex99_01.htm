OGE Energy Corp. reports third quarter 2026 results

OKLAHOMA CITY — OGE Energy Corp. (NYSE: OGE), the parent company of Oklahoma Gas and Electric Company ("OG&E"), today reported earnings of $1.52 per diluted share during the three months that ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.14 per diluted share in the third quarter 2025.

 

•
OG&E, a regulated electric company, contributed earnings of $1.54 per diluted share in the third quarter, compared to earnings of $1.20 per diluted share in the third quarter 2025.
•
Other operations, which includes the holding company, contributed a loss of $0.02 per diluted share compared to a loss of $0.06 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025.

"I'm proud of our team for delivering reliable service through record-setting weather conditions while continuing to execute on investments that strengthen the grid and position us to support future growth across our region," said Sean Trauschke, Chairman, President and CEO of OGE Energy Corp.

 

Third Quarter 2026 results

 

OG&E contributed net income of $320.9 million, or $1.54 per diluted share, in the third quarter compared to $242.9 million, or $1.20 per diluted share, in the same period 2025. The increase in net income was driven by stronger sales from significantly warmer weather, partially offset by increased income tax expense.

 

Other Operations resulted in a loss of $4.7 million, or $0.02 per diluted share, in the third quarter compared to a loss of $11.6 million, or $0.06 per diluted share, in the same period 2025. Results were driven by higher income from energy-related investments and lower interest expense.

 

OGE Energy's net income was $316.2 million or $1.52 per diluted share in the third quarter, compared to earnings of $231.3 million, or $1.14 per diluted share, in the same period 2025.

 

2026 Outlook

Due to warmer than normal weather, OGE Energy expects to exceed its original 2026 earnings guidance range. OGE Energy now expects consolidated earnings between $2.62 to $2.68 per average diluted share. The guidance assumes, among other things, normal weather for the remainder of the year. OG&E has significant seasonality in its earnings due to weather on a year-over-year basis.

 

See OGE Energy’s 2025 Form 10-K for other key factors and assumptions underlying its 2026 guidance.

(A)
Degree days are calculated as follows: The high and low degrees of a particular day are added together and then averaged. If the calculated average is above 65 degrees, then the difference between the calculated average and 65 is expressed as cooling degree days, with each degree of difference equaling one cooling degree day. If the calculated average is below 65 degrees, then the difference between the calculated average and 65 is expressed as heating degree days, with each degree of difference equaling one heating degree day. The daily calculations are then totaled for the particular reporting period. The calculation of heating and cooling degree normal days is based on a 30-year average and weighted on a jurisdictional split.
