Mirion Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Revenues for the third quarter increased 24.5% to $277.8 million, compared to $223.1 million in the same period in 2025.
•GAAP net income was $7.8 million in the third quarter, a 151.6% increase compared to GAAP net income of $3.1 million in the same period in 2025; Adjusted EBITDA was $64.8 million in the third quarter, a 23.7% increase from $52.4 million in the same period last year.
•GAAP net earnings per share in the third quarter was $0.03, compared to $0.01 in the same period in 2025. Adjusted earnings per share for the quarter was $0.12, compared to $0.12 in the same period in 2025. The current period calculation of Adjusted EPS includes stock based compensation expense.
•Third quarter orders, excluding the acquisitions of Paragon and the July results of Certrec, were $340 million, a 62% increase from $209 million in the same period last year. Including the acquisitions of Paragon and the July results of Certrec, third quarter orders were $386 million, a 85% increase compared to the same period last year.
•The company revised 2026 total revenue growth, Organic Revenue growth, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, and Adjusted EPS guidance for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026.

Atlanta, GA – October 27, 2026 – Mirion (“we” or the “company”) (NYSE: MIR), a global provider of radiation detection, measurement, analysis, and monitoring solutions to the nuclear, medical, defense, and research end markets, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Third quarter results showcase quarterly orders performance, an inflection in organic revenue growth, and a doubling of year-to-date adjusted free cash flow generation,” commented Mirion’s Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Thomas Logan. “Orders continued to trend higher, up 62% excluding acquisitions, boosted by a 105% increase in nuclear power-related orders. Organic revenue increased 6.5% in the third quarter and we expect mid-to-high teens growth in the fourth quarter. Meanwhile, adjusted free cash flow totaled $47 million in the quarter, or $107 million year-to-date. We expect to reduce net leverage to approximately 2.6x by year-end, reflecting continued strong adjusted free cash flow.”

Logan continued, “Our business is benefiting from today’s reinvestment in the existing nuclear reactor fleet. Mirion has a presence in over 98% of commercial nuclear power plants globally and approximately 80% of our nuclear power-related revenue comes from reactors operating today. Meanwhile, we continue to capture additional opportunities from large-scale new builds and small modular reactors.”

2026 Guidance
Commenting on Mirion’s full year 2026 guidance, Logan said, “We are updating our guidance to reflect increased visibility to full year performance.”

Mirion revised 2026 total revenue growth, Organic Revenue growth, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Free Cash Flow, and Adjusted EPS guidance for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026.

•Revenue growth of approximately 24% – 25%; includes foreign exchange rate and acquisition-related impacts (previously 22.0% – 24.0%).
•Organic Revenue growth of approximately 7% – 8% (previously 5.0% – 7.0%).
•Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $285 million – $292 million; Adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 24.7% – 25.2% (previously $285 million – $300 million and 25.0% - 26.0%, respectively).
•Adjusted Free Cash Flow of approximately $170 million – $185 million; Adjusted Free Cash Flow Conversion of approximately 60% – 63% of Adjusted EBITDA (previously $155 million - $175 million and 54% - 58%, respectively).
•Adjusted EPS of approximately $0.50 – $0.54 per share (previously $0.48 - $0.55 per share).

Additional modeling and guidance assumptions are included in the appendix of the earnings presentation on the Company’s investor relations page.

The Company’s guidance contains forward-looking statements and actual results may differ materially as a result of known and unknown uncertainties and risks, including those set forth below under the heading “Forward-Looking Statements.” In addition, forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures are presented on a non-GAAP basis without reconciliations of such forward-looking non-GAAP measures due to the inherent difficulty in projecting and quantifying the various adjusting items necessary for such reconciliations, such as stock-based compensation expense, amortization and depreciation expense, merger and acquisition activity and purchase accounting adjustments, that have not yet occurred, are out of Mirion’s control, or cannot be reasonably predicted. About Mirion
Mirion (NYSE: MIR) is a global leader in radiation safety, science and medicine, empowering innovations that deliver vital protection while harnessing the transformative potential of ionizing radiation across a diversity of end markets. The Mirion Nuclear & Safety group provides proven radiation safety technologies that operate with precision – for essential work within R&D labs, critical nuclear facilities, and on the front lines. The Mirion Medical group solutions help enhance the delivery and ensure safety in healthcare, powering the fields of Nuclear Medicine, Radiation Therapy QA, Occupational Dosimetry, and Diagnostic Imaging. Headquartered in Atlanta (GA – USA), Mirion employs approximately 3,200 people and operates in 12 countries. Learn more at mirion.com.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to our results determined in accordance with GAAP, we believe the following non-GAAP measures are useful in evaluating our operating performance. We use the following non-GAAP financial information to evaluate our ongoing operations and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We believe that non-GAAP financial information, when taken collectively, may be helpful to investors because it provides consistency and comparability with past financial performance. However, non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only, has limitations as an analytical tool, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. Other companies, including companies in our industry, may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance, all of which could reduce the usefulness of our non-GAAP financial measures as tools for comparison.
Investors are encouraged to review the related GAAP financial measures and the reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures and not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business.
Organic revenues is defined as Revenues excluding the impact of foreign exchange rates as well as mergers, acquisitions and divestitures in the period.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income before interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization adjusted to remove the impact of foreign currency gains and losses, amortization of acquired intangible assets, changes in the fair value of warrants, certain non-operating expenses (restructuring and costs to achieve operational synergies, merger, acquisition and divestiture expenses and IT project implementation expenses), stock-based compensation expense, debt extinguishment and income tax impacts of these adjustments.
Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by Revenue.
Adjusted net income is defined as GAAP net income adjusted for foreign currency gains and losses, amortization of acquired intangible assets, changes in the fair value of warrants, certain non-operating expenses (restructuring and costs to achieve operational synergies, merger, acquisition and divestiture expenses and IT project implementation expenses), stock-based compensation expense (2025 and prior), debt extinguishment and income tax impacts of these adjustments.
Adjusted EPS is defined as adjusted net income divided by weighted average common shares outstanding — basic and diluted.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow is defined as free cash flow adjusted to include the impact of cash used to fund non-operating expenses. We believe that the inclusion of supplementary adjustments to free cash flow applied in presenting adjusted free cash flow is appropriate to provide additional information to investors about our cash flows that management utilizes on an ongoing basis to assess our ability to generate cash for use in acquisitions and other investing and financing activities.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow Conversion is defined as adjusted free cash flow divided by adjusted EBITDA.
Free cash flow is defined as U.S. GAAP net cash provided by operating activities adjusted to include the impact of purchases of property, plant, and equipment, purchases of badges and proceeds from derivative contracts.
Net leverage is defined as Net Debt (debt minus cash and cash equivalents) divided by Adjusted EBITDA plus contributions to Adjusted EBITDA if acquisitions made during the applicable period had been made before the start of the applicable period.

Operating Metrics
Order and orders growth are defined as the amount of revenue earned in a given period and estimated to be earned in future periods from contracts entered into in a given period as compared with such amount for a prior period. Foreign exchange rates are based on the applicable rates as reported for the time period.
