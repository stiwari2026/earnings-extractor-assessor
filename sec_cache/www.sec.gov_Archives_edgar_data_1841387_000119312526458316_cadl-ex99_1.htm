Candel Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Corporate Highlights

•
The Company is in ongoing dialogue with the FDA regarding the submission of a Biologics License Application (BLA) for aglatimagene in prostate cancer. Due to timelines associated with certain assay refinement, the Company currently plans to submit this BLA in the middle of 2027 pending the result of that dialogue.
•
Cash and cash equivalents of $203.1 million, as of September 30, 2026, are expected to be sufficient to fund the Company’s current operating plan into Q2 2028.
•
Three accepted poster presentations, including a late-breaking presentation, at the Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer’s (SITC) 41st Annual Meeting, showcasing new data for aglatimagene besadenovec (aglatimagene) and linoserpaturev, in localized prostate cancer and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), and recurrent high-grade glioma (rHGG), respectively.

o
SITC poster in metastatic non-squamous NSCLC progressing after immunotherapy reported longitudinal immune changes in blood and tumor tissue and additional multi-omic features associated with survival beyond 24 months. The Company also announced publication of the topline results from the phase 2a clinical trial in NSCLC in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer.

NEEDHAM, Mass., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Candel Therapeutics, Inc. (Candel or the Company) (Nasdaq: CADL), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing multimodal immunotherapies to improve outcomes for patients with cancer, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

“During the quarter we continued to execute well and make significant progress across the business, as we build the foundation to potentially become a commercial-stage biopharma company,” said Paul Peter Tak, M.D., Ph.D., FMedSci, President and CEO of Candel. “On the clinical side, we were honored to have had three posters accepted for presentation at SITC, featuring data on aglatimagene in localized prostate cancer and non-small cell lung cancer, and linoserpaturev in recurrent high-grade glioma. Together, the new biomarker data provide mechanistic support for the systemic effect and long-term clinical outcomes observed in our phase 3 trial of aglatimagene in patients with intermediate- to high-risk localized prostate cancer.”

Dr. Tak continued, “On the corporate side, we continued to build commercial-stage company capabilities. We appointed Martine Zimmermann, a regulatory and quality leader with more than 30 years of experience, to our Board, and Maria Ostrovsky, who has supported multiple oncology launches and strategic partnerships, as Candel’s General Counsel. Both bring expertise that is highly relevant as we prepare for BLA submission and potential future launch. We believe we are well positioned to execute our operating plan into Q2 2028.”

Aglatimagene besadenovec – Prostate Cancer
•
The Company continues to advance its BLA readiness work, including Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) and clinical modules.

Biodistribution and shedding data from the ongoing PrTK05 phase 2 trial in patients with intermediate-risk localized prostate cancer remain on track.

The Company announced novel immunological biomarker data from the ongoing phase 2 PrTK05 trial (NCT07332000) in patients with intermediate-risk

 

 

 

localized prostate cancer and presented a late-breaking poster at the SITC 2026 Annual Meeting (November 2026). See link to poster presentation.
•
Key findings included:
•
Increase in CD8+ cytotoxic T cells, central memory T cells, and effector memory T cells (all p<0.05) at the 2-6-week assessments after the first aglatimagene treatment. Increase in activated, proliferating CD8+ Ki67+ GZMB+ T cells (p<0.0001) 2-3 weeks after the first injection that remained above baseline through Weeks 16-18. Importantly, no increase in any of these cell populations was observed after SoC alone.
•
Increase in serum levels of granzyme A, granzyme B, and perforin 2-3 weeks after the first injection of aglatimagene (p<0.05).

Together, these results suggest systemic immune activation following intraprostatic administration of aglatimagene, consistent with previous observations in glioblastoma, pancreatic cancer, and NSCLC.

•
Additional shedding data presented at SITC demonstrated transient detection of aglatimagene vector DNA in peripheral blood after intraprostatic administration of aglatimagene and clearance in urine samples at weeks 16-18. These findings are consistent with transient systemic biodistribution and shedding following administration.
•
The Company presented new biomarker data from its completed phase 3 clinical trial of aglatimagene in patients with localized intermediate- to high-risk prostate cancer at the American Society for Radiation Oncology (ASTRO) Annual Meeting 2026 in September 2026.
•
AI-enabled digital pathology analysis performed on tumor biopsies evaluable for digital analysis (622 pre-treatment and 427 post-treatment collected more than two years after completion of radiation), demonstrated that aglatimagene plus EBRT significantly increased intratumoral lymphocyte infiltration and remodeled the tumor microenvironment compared with placebo plus EBRT. Among those patients with residual tumor, aglatimagene increased lymphocyte infiltration within tumor regions and enhanced lymphocyte-tumor engagement.
•
Aglatimagene significantly increased overall lymphocytic fraction compared to control (p=0.048), with both arms showing immune activation from radiation therapy (p<0.001) (calculated in all

 

 

 

digital-pathology evaluable biopsies; 622 pre-treatment biopsies and 427 post-treatment biopsies analyzed).
•
In paired biopsies from patients with residual tumor (n=108; aglatimagene n=62, control n=46), aglatimagene significantly increased intratumoral lymphocyte fraction (p=0.006), lymphocyte-tumor enrichment (p=0.003), and lymphocyte-tumor mixing (p<0.001).
•
The Company previously reported that two years after treatment, 80% of evaluable aglatimagene patients (n=167) had negative prostate biopsies versus 63% of control patients (n=62) (p=0.0018).
•
Data from the PrTK03 trial demonstrated that with follow-up through March 15, 2026 (median, 58.0 months), patients with intermediate-risk disease treated with aglatimagene plus EBRT experienced a 41% reduction in the risk of prostate cancer recurrence or prostate cancer-specific death versus placebo plus EBRT (p=0.0034). As previously described, exploratory, descriptive analyses after extended follow-up of these patients showed 52% improvement in the time to biochemical failure, 49% reduction in the time to salvage anticancer therapy, and 90% improvement in the time to metastasis after aglatimagene treatment vs. placebo.
•
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) previously granted Fast Track Designation and Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy Designation to aglatimagene for the treatment of localized prostate cancer. The phase 3 clinical trial of aglatimagene in localized prostate cancer was conducted under a Special Protocol Assessment with respect to certain aspects of the study design, agreed with the FDA.

Aglatimagene besadenovec – Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
•
The Company announced the publication of the phase 2a clinical trial results (NCT04495153) of aglatimagene plus valacyclovir in patients with stage III/IV NSCLC who had shown an inadequate response to immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICI) in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer.
•
The trial demonstrated a median overall survival (mOS) of 24.5 months in the evaluable population, substantially exceeding historical benchmarks for standard-of-care docetaxel chemotherapy in this patient population.
•
The Company presented additional data at the SITC 2026 Annual Meeting, during a poster presentation titled, “Multi-omics analyses reveal intratumoral and

 

 

 

systemic immune activation to aglatimagene besadenovec + valacyclovir related to improved survival in patients with progressive NSCLC despite ICI treatment.”
•
In progressive non-squamous NSCLC, longitudinal immune changes in peripheral blood and tumor tissue and specific multi-omic features were associated with survival beyond 24 months.
•
Integrated biomarker analyses demonstrated broadening of the T-cell repertoire diversity over time, alongside enhanced intratumoral immune activity, including increased tissue expression of genes associated with cytotoxic T-cell activation. Increases in CD40L, NCR1, CXCL5 and granzyme B, as well as antigen-presenting cell- and monocyte/macrophage-rich tumor niches that occurred after aglatimagene treatment were associated with longer survival.
•
As previously announced, the Company initiated the first clinical trial site of the global pivotal phase 3 clinical trial (AURORA) (NCT07660094), which is evaluating aglatimagene plus valacyclovir in combination with continued pembrolizumab in patients with metastatic non-squamous NSCLC whose disease has progressed despite treatment with pembrolizumab and platinum-based chemotherapy.
•
The randomized, open-label AURORA trial is expected to enroll patients with metastatic stage IV non-squamous NSCLC across approximately 150 sites worldwide, randomized 1:1 to receive either two courses of aglatimagene plus valacyclovir with continued pembrolizumab or standard-of-care docetaxel chemotherapy.
•
The study’s primary endpoint is overall survival, with secondary endpoints including safety and quality-of-life assessments (NSCLC-SAQ and EORTC QLQ-30).
•
The FDA previously granted Fast Track Designation to aglatimagene for the treatment of NSCLC.
•
Linoserpaturev - Recurrent high-grade glioma
•
The Company presented updated overall survival data from the single-administration and repeat-dosing cohorts of its phase 1b clinical trial of linoserpaturev (NCT03152318) in patients with rHGG in a poster presentation at the SITC 2026 Annual Meeting.

 

 

 

•
In Arms A+B, 50 patients received 51 intratumoral injections, including one patient who received two injections. mOS was 12.0 months (95% CI, 8.3–14.4), with a median follow-up of 68.6 months. Durable long-term survival was observed in a subset of patients, with 11 of 50 patients (22%) alive at 18 months, 8 of 50 (16%) at 24 months, 5 of 50 (10%) at 36 months, and 2 of 50 (4%) at 48 months, as of the August 14, 2026 data cutoff.
•
In Arm C, 12 patients received repeated administrations of linoserpaturev, with up to six administrations. mOS was 14.3 months (95% CI, 8.9 months to not reached) with a median follow-up of 15.3 months. Five of 12 patients remained alive at efficacy cutoff on July 13, 2026. The Arm C survival data remain immature, with six patients having only recently initiated treatment and therefore having limited follow-up at the data cutoff.
•
The Company also presented data demonstrating that linoserpaturev could potentially be combined with vascular endothelial growth factor blockade to overcome hypoxia-associated immune suppression in glioblastoma.

Financial Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses were $15.6 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $8.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher clinical trial and manufacturing costs, in support of the Company’s aglatimagene programs, and an increase in employee-related expenses. Research and development expenses included a non-cash stock compensation expense of $1.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, as compared to a non-cash stock compensation expense of $0.5 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Based on current operating plans, the Company expects that its existing cash and cash equivalents, as of September 30, 2026, will be sufficient to fund operations into Q2 2028.

About aglatimagene besadenovec

Aglatimagene, Candel’s most advanced multimodal biological immunotherapy candidate, is an investigational, off-the-shelf, replication-defective adenovirus designed to deliver the herpes simplex virus thymidine kinase (HSV-tk) gene to a patient’s tumor. After intratumoral administration, HSV-tk enzyme activity results in conversion of prodrug (valacyclovir) into deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA)-incorporating nucleotide analogs, leading to immunogenic cell death in cells exhibiting DNA damage and proliferating cells, with subsequent release of a variety of tumor (neo)antigens in the tumor microenvironment. At the same time, the adenoviral serotype 5 capsid proteins promote inflammation through the induction of expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines, chemokines, and adhesion molecules. Together, this regimen is designed to induce an individualized and specific CD8+ T cell-mediated response against the injected tumor and uninjected distant metastases for broad anti-tumor activity, based on in situ immunization against a variety of tumor antigens. Aglatimagene has the potential to treat a broad range of solid tumors.

 

 

 

The Company completed successful phase 2a clinical trials of aglatimagene in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with results published in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer, and pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), and a pivotal, placebo-controlled, phase 3 clinical trial of aglatimagene in localized prostate cancer, conducted under a Special Protocol Assessment agreed with the FDA and published in The Lancet Oncology. The FDA also granted Fast Track Designation and Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy Designation to aglatimagene for the treatment of newly diagnosed localized prostate cancer in patients with intermediate- to high-risk disease, Fast Track Designation in NSCLC, and both Fast Track Designation and Orphan Drug Designation to aglatimagene for the treatment of PDAC. More than 1,000 patients have been dosed with aglatimagene in clinical trials with a favorable tolerability profile to date, supporting the potential for use with standard of care, when indicated. Aglatimagene is currently not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or any other regulatory authority for any use.

About linoserpaturev

Linoserpaturev is a first-in-class, replication-competent, next-generation oncolytic herpes simplex virus-1 (HSV-1) immunotherapy candidate designed for dual activity for oncolysis and immune activation in a single therapeutic. In October 2023, the Company announced that Nature published results from the ongoing clinical trial where linoserpaturev was reported to be generally well-tolerated with no dose-limiting toxicity. In the clinical trial, the investigators observed improved median overall survival compared to historical controls after a single linoserpaturev injection in this therapy-resistant condition.1 The Company and academic collaborators are currently supported by Break Through Cancer to evaluate the effects of repeated linoserpaturev injections in patients with recurrent glioblastoma in an expansion cohort from the phase 1b clinical trial. In October 2025, an original manuscript, published in Science Translational Medicine reported collected findings from a comprehensive analysis of 97 serial tumor biopsies collected from two patients treated with repeated administrations of linoserpaturev in arm C. Lastly, in February 2026, Cell published an original manuscript showing that a single linoserpaturev treatment triggered persistent T-cell infiltration and antitumor immunity against glioblastoma, with key immune features associated with longer survival. Linoserpaturev previously received Fast Track Designation and Orphan Drug Designation for the treatment of recurrent high-grade glioma (rHGG) from the FDA.

About Candel Therapeutics

Candel is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing off-the-shelf multimodal biological immunotherapies that elicit an individualized, systemic anti-tumor immune response to help patients fight cancer. Candel has established two clinical-stage multimodal biological immunotherapy platforms based on novel, genetically

 

 

 

modified adenovirus and herpes simplex virus (HSV) gene constructs, respectively. Aglatimagene besadenovec (aglatimagene) is the lead product candidate from the adenovirus platform and is currently in an ongoing phase 3 clinical trial in NSCLC. Linoserpaturev is the lead product candidate from the HSV platform and is currently in an ongoing phase 1b clinical trial in rHGG. Finally, Candel’s enLIGHTEN™ Discovery Platform is a systematic, iterative HSV-based discovery platform leveraging human biology and advanced analytics to create new viral immunotherapies for solid tumors.
