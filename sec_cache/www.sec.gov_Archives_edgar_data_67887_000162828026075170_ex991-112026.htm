Moog Inc. Delivers Record Fiscal 2026 Results;
Guides Another Strong Year for Fiscal 2027

East Aurora, NY -- Moog Inc. (NYSE: MOG.A and MOG.B), a worldwide designer, manufacturer and systems integrator of high-performance precision motion and fluid controls and control systems, today reported record fiscal 2026 results for sales, adjusted segment operating margin, adjusted diluted net earnings per share and free cash flow, along with a strong fiscal fourth quarter.

“Fiscal 2026 was a milestone year for the company. We delivered record results and achieved the goals we established at our 2023 Investor Day," said Pat Roche, CEO. "These results reflect our employees' dedication to improving how we operate and better serving our customers. I'm proud of what we accomplished in our 75th anniversary year and confident in our ability to deliver sustained, profitable growth and long-term value creation."

Quarter Segment Results
Sales in the fourth quarter of 2026 were $1.3 billion, an increase of 20% compared to the fourth quarter of 2025. Space and Defense sales increased 27% to $389 million, reflecting broad-based defense demand including demand for missile controls and for spacecraft components. Military Aircraft sales increased 20% to $283 million, driven by higher OEM activity on production and funded development programs. Commercial Aircraft sales increased 13% to $286 million, driven by growth in aftermarket and higher OEM production levels. Industrial sales increased 17% to $297 million, driven by strong demand for data center cooling pumps, as well as the timing of medical orders.

Segment operating margin in the fourth quarter of 2026 was 14.2%, an increase of 230 basis points compared to the fourth quarter of 2025. Space and Defense operating margin increased 340 basis points to 13.6% due to the absence of the prior year's legal settlement charges. Industrial operating margin increased 190 basis points to 14.6% due to the efficiencies from scaling to meet higher demand for data center cooling pumps. Military Aircraft operating margin increased 170 basis points to 15.7% driven by pricing and favorable mix. Commercial Aircraft operating margin increased 150 basis points to 12.9% due to the gain on the sale of a non-core business.

Adjusted segment operating margin in the fourth quarter of 2026 expanded 40 basis points to 14.1% compared to the fourth quarter of 2025. The fourth quarter of 2025 adjusted segment operating margin excludes $18 million of charges, primarily related to the legal settlement. Space and Defense adjusted margin decreased 140 basis points to 13.7% as increased product development, business capture and operational readiness investments were partially offset by profitable sales growth. Commercial Aircraft adjusted margin decreased 50 basis points to 10.9% due to an unfavorable sales mix. Adjusted segment operating margins in Industrial and Military Aircraft increased 240 basis points and 160 basis points, respectively.

Year Segment Results
Sales for fiscal 2026 were $4.5 billion, an increase of 17% compared to fiscal 2025. Space and Defense sales increased 22% to $1.4 billion, reflecting broad-based defense demand including demand for missile controls, ground vehicles and spacecraft products. Military Aircraft sales increased 14% to $1.0 billion, driven by strong aftermarket and OEM activity, including peak levels of development on the MV-75 program. Commercial Aircraft sales increased 17% to $1.1 billion, driven by higher volume and pricing on various major production programs, as well as strong aftermarket sales. Industrial sales increased 15% to $1.1 billion, driven by strong demand for data center cooling pumps, as well as for medical applications.

Segment operating margin for fiscal 2026 was 13.9%, an increase of 230 basis points compared to fiscal 2025. Industrial operating margin increased 350 basis points to 14.8%, primarily driven by the growing data center cooling pump business, the tariff refund and lower amounts of simplification and other charges. Military Aircraft operating margin expanded 280 basis points to 13.9% driven by favorable sales mix, pricing and by the absence of the prior year’s product development termination charge. Space and Defense operating margin increased 230 basis points to 14.1% due to the absence of the prior year's legal settlement charges and due to profitable sales growth. Commercial Aircraft operating margin increased 20 basis points to 12.6%. Significant pricing benefits, the tariff refund and a gain from a non-core business sale were mostly offset by an unfavorable sales mix, a full year's worth of tariff expense and the absence of the gain on the prior year's non-core product line sale.

Adjusted segment operating margins for fiscal 2026 and 2025 exclude net charges of $17 million and $52 million, respectively. Excluding these items, adjusted segment operating margin in fiscal 2026 expanded 120 basis points to 14.2% compared to fiscal 2025. Adjusted segment operating margins in Industrial, Military Aircraft, and Space and Defense increased 250 basis points, 180 basis points and 120 basis points, respectively, while Commercial Aircraft decreased 30 basis points.

Income Tax Expense
The effective tax rate for fiscal 2026 includes income tax benefits related to U.S. federal research credits of $35 million attributable to prior years. We also recognized an income tax benefit of $8 million related to legal entity simplification initiatives. Adjusted 2026 net earnings and adjusted net earnings per share exclude both the $35 million and $8 million income tax benefits.

Free Cash Flow Results
Free cash flow for fiscal 2026 was $307 million, an increase of $179 million compared to 2025. Free cash flow improved due to higher earnings. In addition, working capital efficiency improved, as reflected in lower net working capital as a percentage of sales compared with the prior year. Capital expenditures were $136 million, relatively unchanged.
