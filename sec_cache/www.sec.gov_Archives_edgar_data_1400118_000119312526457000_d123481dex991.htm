Sagimet Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Updates

 

•  

Registrational U.S. AURORA Phase 3 clinical trial underway for denifanstat in moderate to severe acne, with topline data anticipated in the second half of 2027

 

•  

Positive denifanstat Phase 3 clinical trial results in China from license partner Ascletis, and continued improvement and sustained efficacy across all efficacy endpoints through 52 weeks in the open-label extension clinical trial, presented at EADV 2026 and Fall Clinical Dermatology 2026

 

•  

Completed underwritten offering of Series A Common Stock and pre-funded warrants in October, with gross proceeds of $115 million

 

•  

Pro forma cash and investments balance of approximately $356 million as of September 30, expected to fund current operations through mid-2029 including through readout of data from the denifanstat AURORA Phase 3 clinical trial in moderate to severe acne and potential NDA submission

FOSTER CITY, Calif., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Sagimet Biosciences Inc. (Nasdaq: SGMT), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing inhibitors of fatty acid synthase (FASN), a key lipid synthesis regulator, for acne, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided corporate updates.

“Building on the positive Phase 3 results from our license partner Ascletis, we have initiated our pivotal U.S. AURORA Phase 3 clinical trial,” said David Happel, Chief Executive Officer of Sagimet. “With our October equity financing, we are well positioned to fund our programs through mid-2029, including the anticipated Phase 3 denifanstat data readout and the potential submission of an NDA. If approved, denifanstat would be the first oral acne treatment with a novel mechanism of action approved by the FDA in more than 40 years, offering a new treatment option for approximately 10 million Americans living with moderate to severe acne.”

Recent Corporate Highlights

 

•  

AURORA Phase 3 clinical trial: In October 2026, Sagimet announced that the first patient had been dosed in its AURORA registrational U.S. Phase 3 clinical trial of once-daily denifanstat in adults and adolescents aged 12 years and older with moderate to severe acne. The Company plans to enroll approximately 800 patients in AURORA, including approximately 450 adolescents aged 12 to 17 years. The trial is intended to support a potential U.S. New Drug Application (NDA), with topline data expected in the second half of 2027.

•  

Equity financing: In October 2026, Sagimet completed an underwritten offering of Series A common stock and pre-funded warrants, resulting in approximately $115.0 million in gross proceeds, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses.

 

•  

TVB-3567: The single- and multiple-ascending dose and food effect parts of the first-in-human Phase 1 clinical trial of FASN inhibitor TVB-3567 completed dosing and TVB-3567 was generally well tolerated. Part D of the trial evaluating pharmacodynamic biomarkers in acne patients is ongoing. Phase 2 doses have been identified, and the Company anticipates initiating the Phase 2 clinical trial of TVB-3567 in moderate to severe acne patients before the end of 2026.

 

•  

KOL event: On September 30, 2026, Sagimet hosted a virtual key opinion leader (KOL) event featuring Julie Harper, MD, Founding Director and past President of the American Acne and Rosacea Society. During this event, the Company and Dr. Harper reviewed the 52-week data from license partner Ascletis’ Phase 3 open-label extension clinical trial of denifanstat in moderate to severe acne vulgaris in China and provided an update on Sagimet’s U.S. AURORA Phase 3 clinical trial of denifanstat for the treatment of moderate to severe acne (here).

Publications and Presentations

 

•  

EADV 2026: In September 2026, Sagimet announced positive full 52-week results from Ascletis’ Phase 3 open-label extension clinical trial of denifanstat in moderate to severe acne, which were presented by Ascletis at the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV) Congress. Denifanstat showed progressive and sustained improvements across all efficacy endpoints in the open-label extension, including Investigator’s Global Assessment (IGA) success with a response rate of 57%, 72% reduction in total lesions, and 77% reduction in inflammatory lesions, and was generally well-tolerated, with no adverse event-related permanent discontinuations (here).

 

•  

Fall Clinical Dermatology 2026: In October 2026, Sagimet presented the positive 52-week results from Ascletis’ Phase 3 open-label extension trial, previously presented by Ascletis at EADV 2026, together with mechanistic data supporting denifanstat’s differentiated approach to targeting key aspects of acne pathogenesis: sebum production and inflammation (here).

About the AURORA Phase 3 Clinical Trial

AURORA (NCT07863193) is a multi-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 clinical trial of denifanstat in moderate to severe acne. AURORA is anticipated to enroll approximately 800 U.S. patients aged 12 years and older, of which 450 are expected to be adolescents aged 12 to 17 years. Patients will be randomized 2:1 to receive denifanstat 50 mg or placebo once daily for 12 weeks. The trial will have three co-primary endpoints that will be assessed at week 12: the proportion of patients achieving treatment success in a global assessment score, defined as at least a 2-point reduction from baseline with a score of 0 (clear) or 1 (almost clear); absolute change in inflammatory skin lesion counts from baseline; and absolute change in non-inflammatory skin lesion counts from baseline. A subset of approximately 530 patients completing the double-blind period will be eligible to enter a 40-week open-label extension evaluating the long-term safety of denifanstat.

About Denifanstat

Denifanstat is an oral, once-daily FASN inhibitor in development for the treatment of moderate to severe acne. In trials conducted by Sagimet’s license partner, Ascletis BioScience Co. Ltd. (Ascletis) in China, denifanstat met all primary and secondary endpoints in a 12-week randomized, double-blind Phase 3 clinical trial in moderate to severe acne vulgaris and was generally well-tolerated and showed improvements in all efficacy endpoints measured at 52 weeks (secondary endpoints of the trial) in an open-label extension clinical trial evaluating denifanstat’s long-term safety. Denifanstat is being developed by Ascletis as ASC40 for acne in China and by Sagimet in the rest of world.

About Acne

Acne is one of the most common skin conditions in the U.S., with approximately 50 million Americans affected annually and more than 6.8 million seeking medical treatment for acne each year. Acne affects around 85% of persons between the ages of 12 and 24. Moderate to severe acne accounts for 20% of acne sufferers, or approximately 10 million people in the U.S. annually. There is no cure for acne, and due to its pathology, most patients require chronic management and multiple annual courses of treatment for flare control.

About Sagimet Biosciences

Sagimet is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company advancing a portfolio of novel fatty acid synthase (FASN) inhibitors. FASN is a key regulator of the de novo lipogenesis pathway, which is overactive in acne and can cause elevated sebum and inflammation. FASN is also implicated in other diseases, including MASH and certain FASN-dependent tumor types. Denifanstat, Sagimet’s lead drug candidate, is an oral, once-daily FASN inhibitor currently being evaluated in a U.S. Phase 3 registrational clinical trial for moderate to severe acne.
