EXHIBIT 99.1

Solésence Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Third quarter revenue increases 38% year-over-year to $20.2 million

Third quarter net income of $3.7 million highest in company’s history

Company to host conference call today at 4:00 p.m. CT, 5:00 p.m. ET

ROMEOVILLE, Ill., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Solésence, Inc. (Nasdaq: SLSN), a leader in scientifically-driven health care solutions across beauty and life science categories, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“We’re very pleased that Solésence delivered solid top line growth as well as steady operational and strategic progress in the third quarter, driven by key milestones across our Transform and Transcend framework,” said Kevin Cureton, President and CEO. “Most notably, we reached a major turning point by completing the primary investment and implementation phase of our first pillar, operational excellence. This elevated capacity and efficiency position us to meet robust demand from our brand partners today while seamlessly scaling for tomorrow. Paired with our continued innovation in novel formulations and expanding global presence, Solésence is uniquely positioned to deliver long-term, profitable growth and lead the market in meeting evolving consumer needs.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

  • Third quarter revenue was $20.2 million, compared to $14.6 million for the same period in 2025.
  • Gross profit in the third quarter was $7.9 million, compared to $3.5 million for the same period in 2025.
  • Gross margin in the third quarter was 39.3%, compared to 23.8% for the same period in 2025.
  • Net income for the third quarter was $3.7 million, compared to a net loss of $1.0 million for the same period in 2025.

"By pairing record revenue and net income with strong margin expansion, we have positioned Solésence for sustained growth," said Laura Riffner, Chief Financial Officer. "The operational and financial improvements achieved throughout 2026 provide a strong foundation as we look ahead to 2027."

About Solésence, Inc.
Solésence, Inc. (Nasdaq: SLSN), is a leader in scientifically-driven health care solutions across beauty and life science categories. With a mission to deliver joy through innovation, inclusivity and the science of beautiful skin, we have redefined mineral-based sun protection by maximizing transparency, effectiveness, aesthetics, and wearability — empowering individuals to embrace beauty on their own terms.
