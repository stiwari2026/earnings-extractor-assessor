Walker & Dunlop Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

​

BETHESDA, MD – NOVEMBER 5, 2026 – Walker & Dunlop, Inc. (NYSE: WD) (the “Company,” “Walker & Dunlop” or “W&D”) reported third quarter 2026 financial results.

​

•

Net loss of $37.3 million and diluted loss per share of $1.15 primarily reflect charges associated with the accelerated resolution of legacy loan repurchases and indemnifications. These charges reflect deliberate actions to accelerate the disposition of repurchased and indemnified assets related to the GSE investigations, which are now completed and are described in more detail below

•

Total revenues of $298.4 million declined 12% from Q3’25, in line with the decrease in transaction volumes, and primarily reflecting a greater mix of brokered transactions relative to GSE lending. W&D’s year-to-date GSE market share increased 290 basis points, to 13.7%, from the prior-year period

•

Adjusted core EPS(1) was $0.92 and declined 25% compared to the prior year period due primarily to lower revenue and increased costs within the repurchased portfolio; these costs are expected to be eliminated by early 2027

•

The servicing portfolio increased to $146.6 billion as of September 30, 2026, up 5% from the prior-year period, reinforcing the stability of W&D’s recurring revenue base, cash flow and the strength of its platform

​

“Q3 2026 saw an 84 basis point increase in the Ten-Year Treasury rate which increased volatility and slowed down capital markets activity across the country. Despite these headwinds, our revenues remained resilient, with total debt financing volume down only 5% from Q3 2025," said Willy Walker, Chairman and CEO. "During the quarter we received notice from Fannie Mae that they have completed their fraud review of Walker & Dunlop, similar to Freddie Mac in Q2 2026, and we do not expect any further buybacks or indemnifications related to these investigations going forward. Importantly, we are taking action to address the legacy credit issues associated with these matters and put them firmly behind us. We reclassified all of the repurchased assets and non-performing notes to "held for sale," recognized a significant charge to Q3 earnings, and intend to dispose of these assets by early 2027. While these actions have a meaningful near-term impact on earnings, they reflect our commitment to proactively resolving credit issues, reducing risk on our balance sheet, and strengthening our financial position for the future. We believe that the completion of the GSE investigations and the sale of the repurchased loans, allows Walker & Dunlop to move beyond the borrower fraud issues that have consumed us, and our financial performance, for the past two years.”

​

Walker continued, “We believe there is a tremendous opportunity ahead as commercial real estate owners navigate the upcoming wave of debt maturities and evaluate whether to refinance, sell, or recapitalize their properties. We have refined our strategy to focus on the multifamily market where W&D has a massive brand and there is nearly $1 trillion of maturing loans over the next four years. We are leveraging technology to improve productivity, strengthen client relationships, and reduce our fixed cost structure, and underwent a reduction in force and reorganization in Q3. While higher interest rates and lower asset values present significant challenges for property owners, as a scaled service provider with an enormous tangible addressable market in our company's core competency, we now have the ability to execute, grow, and get back to the outstanding shareholder returns Walker & Dunlop has been known for.”

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

To supplement our financial statements presented in accordance with United States generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company uses adjusted EBITDA, adjusted core net income, and adjusted core EPS, which are non-GAAP financial measures. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. When analyzing our operating performance, readers should use adjusted EBITDA, adjusted core net income, and adjusted core EPS in addition to, and not as an alternative for, net income and diluted EPS.

Adjusted core net income and adjusted core EPS represent net income adjusted for amortization and depreciation, provision (benefit) for credit losses, net write-offs based on the final resolution of the defaulted loans or collateral, the fair value of expected net cash flows from servicing, net of guaranty obligation, the income statement impact from periodic revaluation and accretion associated with contingent consideration liabilities related to acquired companies, goodwill impairment, loan repurchase losses and other adjustments. Adjusted EBITDA represents net income before income taxes, interest expense on our corporate debt, and amortization and depreciation, adjusted for provision (benefit) for credit losses, net write-offs based on the final resolution of the defaulted loans or collateral, loan repurchase losses, stock-based compensation, the fair value of expected net cash flows from servicing, net of guaranty obligation,
