Orion Reports 9% Revenue Increase in Second Quarter FY2027;

Reiterates Revenue Expectation of $100M-$102M for Full Fiscal Year

 

Manitowoc, WI – November 5, 2026 – Orion Energy Systems, Inc. (NASDAQ: OESX) (Orion Lighting), a provider of energy-efficient LED lighting, electric vehicle (EV) charging stations and maintenance services solutions, today reported results for its fiscal 2027 second quarter (Q2’27) ended September 30, 2026.

 

Orion’s Q2’27 revenue increased to $21.7M versus $19.9M in Q2’26, while Q2’27 gross margin was 28.7% versus 31.0% in Q2’26. The Company reported a net loss of $0.8M in Q2’27, compared to a net loss of $0.6M in Q2’26. The Company’s adjusted EBITDA was positive in Q2 FY’27 — marking its eighth consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA.

 

Orion reiterated its previously increased full fiscal year expectations of positive adjusted EBITDA on revenue of between $100 million and $102 million in FY’27, which began April 1, 2026.

 

The Company noted that it expects a strong second half, with significant projects to be delivered in the third and fourth quarters. These orders are coming from a broad array of customers and include retailers, manufacturers, logistics providers, data centers and government projects.

 

“Orion enters the second half of FY’27 accelerating our initiatives to achieve diversified growth, sustainable margins and market expansion,” said Orion’s Chief Executive Officer, Sally Washlow. “Whether it’s rapidly growing engagements with major retail chains and logistics leaders, more and more campuses for hyper-scale data-center operators or expanding incumbencies with global automakers, Orion continues on a trajectory to achieve our profitable growth expectations for the current fiscal year.”

 

Orion is scheduled to discuss these results in an investor call today at 10:00 a.m. ET (details below).

Q2’27 Business Highlights: Commentary from CEO Sally Washlow

 

Orion’s second-quarter results and full-year expectations illuminate a continually strengthening sales funnel and expanding wallet share within enterprise customers. They also highlight the power of Orion’s proprietary supply chain, cited as a unique attribute by customers ranging from data-center operators to leading retail chains and logistics providers. Combined with Orion’s diversified-growth initiative and ongoing cost containment, these factors indicate positive prospects for achieving its recently increased guidance for the full fiscal year of FY2027.

 

Orion’s Q2’27 included a number of additional milestones:

 

— Increased Guidance for FY’27: Orion announced on September 15 that it expects to achieve total revenue of $100 million to $102 million for FY 2027, representing an increase from its previous outlook of $95 million to $97 million. As previously announced, Orion reiterated that it expected FY’Q2 revenues to decrease compared to the recently announced FY’Q1 revenue of $25.7M. The Company also said that it continues to expect to achieve positive adjusted EBITDA for the fiscal year as a whole.

 

— Another Data Center Win: Orion announced an additional deployment of its MPHL2 LED Lighting solution for one of the world’s largest data-center operators. The multimillion-dollar order followed June’s announcement of Orion’s first multimillion-dollar deployment for the global hyperscaler.

 

The customizable MPHL2 LED Lighting solution, manufactured in Orion’s home state of Wisconsin, is capable of delivering higher energy efficiency, resiliency and decarbonization in LED lighting to thousands of data centers in the current hyperscaler building boom. The high efficiency linear lighting fixture is designed and built to accommodate nearly every data center’s floor plan while reducing the energy usage for lighting

 

— $10M-$15M New Retailer Engagement. Orion announced that a longtime customer has made a follow-on commitment to the Company for a new LED lighting project estimated to represent revenue of $10 million to $15 million.

 

This latest commitment involves retrofitting interior LED Lighting and IoT system installations. Orion maintains that this most recent expansion of work demonstrates the confidence that Orion has earned with this leading retailer over the course of numerous projects spanning several years.

 

— Additionally, Orion/Voltrek continued to navigate a U.S. EV Charging Infrastructure environment that continues to evolve. Orion/Voltrek maintains numerous multi-year customer relationships as they progress through historically reliable funding cycles. The Company also foresees increased inclusion of EV Charging infrastructure in its LED Lighting engagements, typified by Orion’s recent announcement of related LED Lighting and EV Charging deployments for a leading global automaker.

 

Q2’27 Financial Results

 

Orion’s Q2’27 revenue was $21.7M versus $19.9M in Q2’26, while Q2’27 gross margin was 28.7% versus 31.0% in Q2’26. The Company reported a net loss of $0.8M in Q2’27 versus a net loss of $0.6M in Q2’26. The Company achieved Q2’27 adjusted EBITDA of $0.0M — marking its eighth consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA — compared to adjusted EBITDA of $0.5M in Q2’26.

 

Orion also reported the following Q2’27 segment performance:

•
LED lighting revenue was approximately $14.9M in Q2’27, compared to $10.7M in Q2’26, reflecting increased large project activity.
•
Maintenance services revenue was $4.0M in Q2’27, compared to $4.5M in Q2’26
•
EV charging solutions revenue was $2.8M in Q2’27, compared to $4.8M in Q2’26, reflecting the variability in timing of larger projects related to the current U.S. market uncertainty regarding the near-term scope, pace and funding availability for EV charging projects.
•
Orion’s Q2’27 gross margin was 28.7% versus 31.0% in Q2’26, primarily due to a mix shift in the lighting segment during the quarter. Total operating expenses increased to $7.0M in Q2’27 from $6.4M in Q2’26, reflecting an increase in commissions and equity compensation.

Primarily reflecting lower gross margin and increased commissions and equity compensation, Orion reported a net loss of $0.8M in Q2’27, or $(0.20) per share, versus a net loss of $0.6M, or $(0.17) per share, in Q2’26. Orion’s adjusted EBITDA decreased to $0.0M in Q2’27 compared to $0.5M in Q2’26, reflecting relatively strong performance in the face of temporary margin headwinds.

 

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

Orion ended the quarter with current assets of $35.9M, including $3.7M of cash, $15.0M of accounts receivable, $5.7M of revenue earned but not billed, and $10.0M of inventories. Net of current liabilities, working capital was $15.4M at September 30, 2026, compared to $8.1M at September 30, 2025. Orion’s financial liquidity at September 30, 2026, was $16.2M as compared to $13.5M at September 30, 2025. As previously reported, Orion recently extended the maturity date of its revolving credit facility from June 30, 2027, to June 30, 2030.

 

About Orion Energy Systems

Orion provides energy efficiency and clean tech solutions, including LED lighting and controls, electric vehicle (EV) charging solutions, and maintenance services. Orion specializes in turnkey design-through-installation solutions for large national customers as well as projects through ESCO and distribution partners, with a commitment to helping customers achieve their business and environmental goals with healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions that reduce their carbon footprint and enhance business performance.
