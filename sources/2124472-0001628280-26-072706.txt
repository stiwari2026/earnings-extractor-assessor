Orion180 Insurance Group Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
- Managed Premiums In-Force Grows 72% from Prior-Year Quarter End to $678 million -
- Managed Premiums Written Grows 60% Year-over-Year to $204 million -
- Net Income Increases 32% Year-over-Year to $11 million -
- Completed Initial Public Offering in September 2026 -
MELBOURNE, Fla. — November 10, 2026 — Orion180 Insurance Group Inc. (NASDAQ: OIG) (“Orion180” or the “Company”), a leading provider of flexible, customer-centric homeowners and flood insurance solutions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Managed premiums in-force was $678 million as of September 30, 2026 vs. $394 million as of September 30, 2025.
•Managed premiums written grew 60% to $204 million during the third quarter of 2026 vs. $127 million in the prior-year period.
•New business premiums written increased 62% to $102 million during the third quarter of 2026 vs. $63 million in the prior-year period.
•Policy retention rate was 88% during the third quarter of 2026 vs. 90% in the prior-year period.
•Net income grew 32% to $11 million during the third quarter of 2026 vs. $9 million in the same quarter during the prior year.
•Consolidated adjusted EBITDA increased 65% to $25 million during the third quarter of 2026, compared to $15 million in the prior-year period.
“We delivered an excellent quarter across our key metrics, rapidly growing our managed premiums while significantly increasing our profitability on a year-over-year basis,” said Kenneth Gregg, Chief Executive Officer of Orion180. “In addition, we achieved a major milestone in our Company’s history by successfully completing our initial public offering. We believe our results reflect the strength, differentiation and scalability of the business model we have built over the past eight years.”
“We are well-positioned to produce durable growth on both our top and bottom lines, which is driven by our attractive suite of homeowners solutions - including our flagship FLEX product - as well as our proprietary end-to-end MY180 tech platform, which allows us to seamlessly combine best-in-class underwriting, claims management, and real-time analytic capabilities,” added Mr. Gregg. “As a result, we are able to compound our competitive advantage and continue our growth with underwriting discipline across our growing 14-state footprint. We also continue to develop our MY180 tech platform capabilities to further drive future growth. Moving forward, we are well-positioned to sustainably continue gaining market share while increasing long-term value for shareholders, leading the way as a premier provider of specialty property insurance solutions.”

About Orion180
Orion180 Insurance Group Inc. (Orion180) (NASDAQ: OIG) is a founder-led technology-focused, high growth, high-margin specialty insurance group delivering innovative insurance solutions with a growing footprint across the United States. Since beginning operations in 2018, they have organically grown to become the second largest excess and surplus (“E&S”) lines homeowners insurance provider in the United States by direct written premiums with a presence in 14 states. Their diverse product offerings across E&S and admitted homeowners’ insurance, private flood insurance, and a range of ancillary products are distributed through a network of approximately 17,000 active independent agents as of September 30, 2026.
Orion180’s business is built on four core principles: (i) a relentless focus on innovation, (ii) real-time, data-driven decision making through their proprietary, end-to-end MY180 platform, (iii) ease of doing business, and (iv) long-term partnerships with their distribution providers and reinsurance partners. Headquartered in Melbourne, Florida, Orion180 also has offices in Irving, Texas and Draper, Utah. Learn more at www.orion180.com.

The non-GAAP financial measures are not recognized terms under GAAP and should not be considered as alternatives to the corresponding GAAP measures of financial performance, or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP. Because not all companies use identical calculations, the presentation of the non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies and can differ significantly from company to company. Our management uses these non-GAAP financial measures, in conjunction with GAAP financial measures, as an integral part of managing our business and to, among other things: (i) monitor and evaluate the performance of our business operations and financial performance; (ii) facilitate internal comparisons of the historical operating performance of our business operations; (iii) facilitate external comparisons of the results of our overall business to the historical operating performance of other companies that may have different capital structures and debt levels and different go-to-market models; (iv) review and assess the operating performance of our management team; (v) analyze and evaluate financial and strategic planning decisions regarding future operating investments; and (vi) plan for and prepare future annual operating budgets and determine appropriate levels of operating investments.

Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA and Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA Margin
Consolidated adjusted EBITDA removes non-cash and non-recurring charges that occur in the affected period and provides a basis for measuring our core financial performance against other periods. We define consolidated adjusted EBITDA as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization (i.e., software and loan), and non-recurring charges.
Consolidated adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure used as an analytical indicator to assess business performance. Consolidated adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as a measure of financial performance, liquidity, or cash flows provided by or (used) in operating, investing, and financing activities, as there may be significant factors or trends that it fails to address. Consolidated adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net income (loss), or other financial statement data presented in our consolidated financial statements as an indicator of financial performance. Consolidated adjusted EBITDA is not a measurement determined in accordance with GAAP and is therefore susceptible to varying methods of calculation and this measure, as presented, may not

Segment Results
We have identified two operating segments: Services and Underwriting.
Services segment
The Services segment includes technology-enabled policy servicing operations, which include intercompany revenue earned through service arrangements in place between the Services segment and the Underwriting segment.
We evaluate the performance of the Services segment primarily using Services Adjusted EBITDA as the key performance measure and also using commission and fees revenue, as an additional performance measure.
Commission and fees revenue is a non-GAAP measure and additional services segment performance measure that management uses, which reflects services segment revenue less other income as such items are not directly attributable to the operating performance of the services segment. Management uses this non-GAAP financial measure to monitor operating revenue for the services business. Commission and fees revenue should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to revenue.
Underwriting segment
The Underwriting segment includes a conventional, regulated insurance model focused on risk selection, pricing, and statutory capital management. The segment also includes a segregated account designed to assume reinsurance risk.
Management evaluates the performance of the Underwriting segment using total revenue, net premiums earned, underwriting adjusted EBITDA, and key underwriting ratios, including gross loss ratio, net loss ratio, expense ratio, and combined ratio. These measures collectively provide insight into premium growth, underwriting effectiveness, cost efficiency, and overall profitability of our risk-bearing operations.
