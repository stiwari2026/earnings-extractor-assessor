Global Water Resources Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

PHOENIX, AZ – November 3, 2026 – Global Water Resources, Inc. (NASDAQ: GWRS), a pure-play water resource management company, reported results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Unless otherwise noted, all comparisons are to the corresponding period in the prior year. The company will hold a conference call on Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. Eastern time to discuss the results (see dial-in information below).
Q3 2026 Financial Highlights
•Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 increased $0.8 million or 5.0% year-over-year (YoY) to $16.3 million compared to the same period in 2025. Total revenue for the year-to-date period increased $5.1 million or 12.2% to $47.4 million. The increase in both periods was primarily driven by organic connection growth in active water and wastewater connections, increased consumption revenue from irrigation, residential and multi-family customers, and higher rates. The increase in the year-to-date period was also aided by unregulated revenue recognition of $2.1 million during the second quarter of 2026 and the acquisition of the seven water systems from the City of Tucson in July 2025.
•Net income for the third quarter of 2026 decreased $0.7 million to $1.0 million or $0.03 per diluted share, as compared to net income of $1.7 million or $0.06 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the year-to-date period was $3.4 million or $0.12 per diluted share, as compared to net income of $3.9 million or $0.15 per diluted share in the prior year period. The decrease in both periods was primarily driven by higher depreciation and net interest expense.
•Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, remained largely consistent for the quarter-to-date periods at $7.8 million. Adjusted EBITDA for the year-to-date period increased $1.0 million or 5.0% to $21.4 million (see definition of adjusted EBITDA, and its reconciliation to GAAP, below). The year-to-date increase was primarily driven by higher revenue and partially offset by increased operations and maintenance expense.
•Completed a $10.0 million private placement of common stock and amended its revolving credit facility, increasing borrowing capacity from $20.0 million to $30.0 million.
•Declared three monthly cash dividends of $0.02533 per common share or $0.30396 per common share on an annualized basis.
Q3 2026 Operational Highlights
•Total active service connections at September 30, 2026 increased 2.4% YoY to 69,797.
•Water consumption remained steady at approximately 1.3 billion gallons.
•Invested $6.9 million in Q3 2026 in infrastructure projects to support continued safe and reliable service in our existing utilities.
•GW-Palo Verde filed a motion to withdraw its rate application in accordance with the company’s previously announced settlement agreement, which remains subject to Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) approval. The motion was granted on July 29, 2026. Global Water plans to refile the GW-Palo Verde rate case in 2027 using a 2026 test year.
Subsequent Events
•GW-Santa Cruz received ACC approval of its new water availability fee on October 14, 2026.
•On October 19, 2026, the Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) issued a Recommended Opinion and Order, which recommended that the ACC adopt the settlement agreement with new rates for GW-Santa Cruz effective December 1, 2026.

Management Commentary
“In the third quarter, total revenue increased 5.0% year over year to $16.3 million,” commented Global Water Resources President and CEO Ron Fleming. “Total active service connections increased 2.4% year over year to 69,797.
“We invested $6.9 million during the quarter in infrastructure improvements across our existing utilities. These investments build on our significant 2025 capital program, including recommissioning the Southwest Plant Water Reclamation Facility, and expand the rate base needed to continue safe and reliable service to our customers and communities over the long term.
“As expected, assets placed in service under the 2025 and 2026 capital improvement plans increased depreciation expense and continued to affect near-term net income and earnings per share. Operations and maintenance expense also increased, primarily due to rising medical costs and higher costs to power and operate our newly operational plant.
“At the same time, general and administrative expense remained relatively flat at $4.8 million, compared with $4.9 million in the prior-year quarter, reflecting our continued focus on cost discipline. We remain committed to pursuing long-term earnings growth by combining disciplined expense management with organic growth and appropriate rate recovery.
“We appreciate the ALJ issuing his order and opinion to the ACC recommending adoption of the settlement agreement for our GW-Santa Cruz and GW-Palo Verde utilities, which will be deliberated by the ACC at the open meeting on November 4, 2026. If approved, we expect new rates for GW-Santa Cruz to take effect on December 1, 2026.
“For GW-Palo Verde, the withdrawal of the current rate case provides a clearer path to seeking appropriate recovery using a 2026 test year. We have begun preparing rate reviews for GW-Palo Verde and our three Pima County utilities—GW-Saguaro, GW-Farmers and GW-Ocotillo—which we anticipate filing in the first half of 2027, with estimated new rates in 2028.
“We have also started preliminary planning for the next GW-Santa Cruz rate case, tentatively using a 2027 test year for a 2028 filing and estimated new rates in 2029. Across these proceedings, we are communicating with community stakeholders and ACC staff about the importance of our investments and the customer benefits they provide.
“Several long-term growth drivers support our outlook for Arizona’s Sun Corridor. Earlier this year, TSMC increased its planned Arizona investment by $100 billion to $265 billion, supporting additional semiconductor manufacturing and advanced packaging facilities that are expected to drive job creation and regional development. Arizona’s Ag-to-Urban program also creates a pathway to convert agricultural water rights to municipal use, potentially supporting new housing while reducing groundwater demand. With approximately 384,000 eligible agricultural acres across the Phoenix and Pinal active management areas—including about two-thirds in Pinal County—we believe the program could support sustainable development and long-term demand across our core service areas.
“While the pace of new connection and permit activity has slowed recently, ongoing development throughout the Phoenix metropolitan area—particularly in the City of Maricopa—continues to support organic growth in our active connection base. We believe the decline is temporary, primarily driven by higher mortgage rates and other macroeconomic factors, and that we remain well-positioned to benefit from the region’s long-term population and job growth, housing affordability and transportation improvements such as the State Route 347 widening. By expanding our Total Water Management platform and applying our expertise across our regional service areas and new utilities, we believe we can create lasting value for customers, communities and shareholders.”

Business Strategy
Global Water's near-term growth strategy involves increasing service connections, improving operating efficiencies, and increasing utility rates as approved by the ACC. The company plans to continue aggregating water and wastewater utilities through strategic acquisitions and entity consolidation, which is expected to enable the company and its customers to realize the benefits of consolidation, regionalization, and environmental stewardship.
Connection Rates
As of September 30, 2026, active service connections increased by 1,667 or 2.4% to 69,797 compared to 68,130 at September 30, 2025. The increase in active service connections was due to organic growth in the company’s service areas.
Arizona’s Growth Corridor: Positive Population and Economic Trends
The company continues to experience organic growth exhibited through its year-over-year organic increase in active connections (i.e., exclusive of acquisition related growth) of 2.4% as of September 30, 2026. According to the 2025 U.S. Census estimates, the Phoenix metropolitan statistical area (MSA) is the 10th largest MSA in the U.S. and had an estimated population of 5.2 million, an increase of 7.9% over the 4.8 million people reported in the 2020 Census.
6

Growth in the Phoenix MSA continues as a result of its excellent weather, large and growing universities, a diverse employment base, and low taxes. The Arizona Office of Economic Opportunity predicts that the Phoenix metropolitan area will have a population of 5.7 million people by 2030 and 6.3 million by 2040.
The company’s organic growth continues to be primarily influenced by the comparatively lower cost of housing in the City of Maricopa relative to other areas within the Phoenix MSA. As of September 2026, the median home sales price in the City of Maricopa was 24% lower than in the City of Phoenix. In addition, construction on the State Route 347 Improvement Project began in June 2026, with completion scheduled for 2029. The project represents a transformative investment in regional infrastructure that the company believes will enhance safety, improve mobility and support the continued growth of the City of Maricopa and surrounding areas.
While the pace of new connection and permit activity has slowed recently, ongoing development throughout the Phoenix MSA, particularly in the City of Maricopa, continues to support organic growth, as reflected in the company’s 2.4% year-over-year increase in active connections. Management believes, despite fluctuations in permit projections, the company remains well-positioned to benefit from the anticipated long-term growth of the Phoenix MSA. The company continues to expect a positive long-term outlook based on forecasted performance of job and population growth, as well as indicators of stabilizing construction in the single-family housing market in the Phoenix MSA.

About Global Water Resources
Global Water Resources, Inc. is a leading water resource management company that owns and operates 39 systems which provide water, wastewater, and recycled water service. The company’s service areas are located primarily in growth corridors around metropolitan Phoenix and Tucson. Global Water recycles over 1 billion gallons of water annually with 21.7 billion gallons recycled since 2004.
The company has been recognized for its highly effective implementation of Total Water Management (TWM). TWM is an integrated approach to managing the entire water cycle that involves owning and operating water, wastewater and recycled water utilities within the same geographic area in order to maximize the beneficial use of recycled water. It enables smart water management programs such as remote metering infrastructure and other advanced technologies, rate designs, and incentives that result in real conservation. TWM helps protect water supplies in water-scarce areas experiencing population growth.
Global Water has received numerous industry awards, including national recognition as a ‘Utility of the Future Today’ for its superior water reuse practices by a national consortium of water and conservation organizations led by the Water Environment Federation (WEF). The company also received Cityworks’ Excellence in Departmental Practice

Use of Non-GAAP Measures
This press release contains certain financial measures that are not recognized measures under accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”), including EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per common share. EBITDA is defined for the purposes of this press release as net income before interest, income taxes, depreciation, amortization and accretion. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA excluding the gain or loss related to (i) restricted stock expense related to awards made to employees and the board of directors; (ii) disposal of assets; (iii) ICFA revenue; and (iv) nonrecurring events, as applicable. Adjusted net income and adjusted diluted earnings per common share reflect net income and diluted earnings per common share excluding the gain or loss related to (i) ICFA revenue; (ii) amortization related to ICFA intangible assets; (iii) disposal of assets (iv) nonrecurring events; and (v) the tax effect of these items, as applicable.
Management believes that EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per common share are useful supplemental measures of our operating performance and provide our investors meaningful measures of overall corporate performance. EBITDA is also presented because management believes that it is frequently used by investment analysts, investors, and other interested parties as a measure of financial performance. Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per common share are also presented because management believes that they provide our investors additional measures of our recurring core business. However, non-GAAP measures do not have a standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP, and investors are cautioned that non-GAAP measures, such as EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per common share, should not be construed as an alternative to net income, diluted earnings per common share, or other income statement data (which are determined in accordance with GAAP) as an indicator of our performance or as a measure of liquidity and cash flows. Management's method of calculating EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted net income, and adjusted diluted earnings per common share may differ materially from the method used by other companies and accordingly, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. A reconciliation of EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted net income to net income and a reconciliation of adjusted diluted earnings per common share to diluted earnings per common share, the most comparable GAAP measures, are included in the schedules attached to this press release.
