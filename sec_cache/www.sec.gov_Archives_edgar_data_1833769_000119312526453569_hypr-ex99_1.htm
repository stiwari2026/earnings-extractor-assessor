Hyperfine, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

GUILFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--November 9, 2026--Hyperfine, Inc. (Nasdaq: HYPR), the groundbreaking health technology company that has redefined brain imaging with the first FDA-cleared AI-powered portable MRI system for the brain—the Swoop® system—today announced third quarter 2026 financial results and provided a business update.

“Q3 2026 marked the beginning of a new trajectory for Hyperfine. We delivered record revenue, gross profit, and gross margin, and for the first time quarterly revenue exceeded net cash burn, demonstrating the operating leverage of our business. The combination of AI with accessible brain imaging enables diagnostic insights at scale, sooner, and in more care settings, from the health system to the community. Beyond our current business, I am excited about the opportunities for our Swoop® system in the evolving brain health and longevity fields. This is the new Hyperfine,” said Maria Sainz, President and Chief Executive Officer of Hyperfine, Inc.

Recent Achievements and Business Highlights

•
Completed enrollment in the Contrast PMR study, a key milestone toward expanding the Swoop® system's labeling, with FDA submission remaining on track and expected by the end of 2026.
•
Launched Swoop® system Model 2 in Europe and the UK following CE and UKCA marking, with sales into four international markets, and signed an exclusive distributor agreement for Kuwait and the United Arab Emirates.
•
Received FDA clearance and CE and UKCA marking for the latest Optive AI™ software. The software release improves noise correction, adds guided scanning, and introduces an isotropic T1 sequence.
•
Published NEURO PMR study results in the Journal of Neuroimaging, showing 92% concordance between portable MRI and high-field MRI in blinded review, and 98% concordance when paired with clinical information.
•
Expanded Swoop® system evidence base with new peer-reviewed Stroke and Journal of Neurosurgery publications, adding to more than 180 publications and 280 scientific presentations to date.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were a record $6.0 million, increasing 73% compared to $3.4 million in the third quarter of 2025 and 53% compared to $3.9 million in the second quarter of 2026.
•
Sold 14 commercial Swoop® systems in the third quarter of 2026, increasing 75% compared to 8 sold in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was a record $3.4 million, compared to $1.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing a record 57% gross margin in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 54% gross margin in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Research and development expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $3.9 million, decreasing 3% compared to $4.0 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Sales, marketing, general, and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $6.7 million, relatively flat compared to $6.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $7.3 million, the Company's narrowest quarterly operating loss, an improvement of 19% compared to $8.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue grew 73% and gross profit grew 83% while total operating expenses decreased 1%, reflecting the operating leverage of the business.
•
Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.0 million, equating to a net loss of $0.06 per share, as compared to a net loss of $11.0 million, or a net loss of $0.14 per share, for the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter of 2026 net loss includes a $1.5 million gain from a change in the fair value of warrant liabilities, compared to a $2.3 million loss in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Net cash burn1 for the third quarter of 2026 was $5.6 million, the Company's lowest quarterly net cash burn, compared to $5.9 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $7.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter 2026 revenue exceeded net cash burn for the first time.
•
Cash and cash equivalents were $37.9 million as of September 30, 2026. The Company did not generate any financing proceeds during the third quarter of 2026.

2026 Financial Guidance

Management continues to expect revenue for the full year 2026 to be approximately $20 to $22 million, representing 55% growth at the midpoint as compared to full year 2025.

Management continues to expect net cash burn1 for the full year 2026 to be approximately $26 to $28 million, representing a 10% decline at the midpoint as compared to full year 2025.

About Swoop® Portable MR Imaging® Systems

The Swoop® Portable MR Imaging® systems are U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)-cleared for brain imaging of patients of all ages. They are portable, ultra-low-field magnetic resonance imaging devices for producing images that display the internal structure of the head where full diagnostic examination is not clinically practical. When interpreted by a trained physician, these images provide information that can be useful in determining a diagnosis. The Swoop® system also has CE Mark in the European Union and UKCA Mark in the United Kingdom. The Swoop® system is commercially available in a select number of international markets.

About Hyperfine, Inc.

Hyperfine, Inc. (Nasdaq: HYPR) is the groundbreaking health technology company that has redefined brain imaging with the Swoop® system—the first FDA-cleared, portable, ultra-low-field, magnetic resonance brain imaging system capable of providing imaging at multiple points of professional care. The mission of Hyperfine, Inc. is to revolutionize patient care globally through transformational, accessible, clinically relevant diagnostic imaging. Founded by Dr. Jonathan Rothberg in a technology-based incubator called 4Catalyzer, Hyperfine, Inc. scientists, engineers, and physicists developed the Swoop® system out of a passion for redefining brain imaging methodology and how clinicians can apply accessible diagnostic imaging to patient care. For more information, visit hyperfine.io.
