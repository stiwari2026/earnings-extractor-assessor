Labcorp Reports Strong 2026 Third Quarter Results; Raises Full Year 2026 Guidance

•Results from operations for third quarter 2026 versus third quarter 2025:
◦Revenue: $3.81 billion vs. $3.56 billion, up 6.9%
◦Diluted EPS: $3.22 vs. $3.12, up 3.3%
◦Adjusted EPS: $4.78 vs. $4.18, up 14.5%
•Raised full-year enterprise revenue growth and adjusted EPS guidance:
◦Revenue growth range of 5.9% to 6.7%; up 50 basis points at the midpoint
◦Adjusted EPS range of $18.30 to $18.70; up 18 cents at the midpoint
•Advanced M&A strategy by announcing or closing four transactions; each are expected to be accretive to adjusted EPS in year one and return cost of capital by year three
•Received FDA approval of PGDx elio® tissue complete CDx as a companion diagnostic to help identify patients with advanced melanoma


BURLINGTON, N.C., October 28, 2026 – Labcorp Holdings Inc. (NYSE: LH), a global leader of innovative and comprehensive laboratory services, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and updated its full-year financial guidance.

“Labcorp delivered another strong quarter with 7% revenue growth, continued margin expansion and 14.5% adjusted EPS growth, leading to a raise in our full-year guidance,” said Adam Schechter, Chairman and CEO of Labcorp. “We furthered our leadership in specialty testing by launching innovative tests for advanced melanoma, Alzheimer’s disease, and hepatitis D, while expanding access through strategic acquisitions across our Diagnostics and Biopharma Laboratory Services businesses. As healthcare continues to be transformed by advances in science and technology, personalized medicine, and evolving consumer expectations, Labcorp is well positioned to capitalize on these trends and drive sustainable long-term growth.”




1

Labcorp continues to advance its strategic priorities:

Lead in specialty testing:

•Received FDA approval of PGDx elio® tissue complete CDx as a companion diagnostic to help identify patients with advanced melanoma.
•Announced a collaboration with the Knight Cancer Institute at Oregon Health & Science University on a clinical study evaluating the use of Plasma Detect® Genome MRD Ultra to help identify disease recurrence earlier in patients with muscle-invasive bladder cancer.
•Announced the availability of a new hepatitis D reflex testing option within its comprehensive hepatitis testing portfolio.
•Subsequent to the quarter:
◦launched the FDA-cleared Elecsys® pTau-217 blood test, expanding access to Alzheimer's disease testing in both primary and specialty care settings.
◦announced a collaboration with Waters Corporation that positions Labcorp as the first commercial laboratory to offer the FDA-approved BD Onclarity™ HPV assay across clinician- and self-collected screening options, including in-clinic, patient service center and at-home settings.

Be the partner of choice for health systems and leading pharmaceutical companies:

•Acquired MLM Medical Labs, a leading global central and specialty laboratory provider with operations across the United States, Germany and South Africa.
•Partnered with the National Association of Community Health Centers to strengthen leadership development, support practice improvement and advance community health initiatives across the nation’s Community Health Center network.
•Subsequent to quarter end, signed the following three acquisition agreements; the timing of each of these is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions:
◦an agreement to acquire select assets of BioReference Health’s laboratory testing businesses focused on clinical and women’s health testing services, urology, corrections and certain operational assets.
◦an agreement with Myriad Genetics to acquire their Prenatal Health testing business, which includes the Prequel®, Foresight, SneakPeek®, and FirstGene® tests.
◦and an agreement to acquire Millennium Health, an accredited specialty laboratory with more than 15 years of experience in drug testing and surveillance.

Collectively, these acquisitions and partnerships expand Labcorp's capabilities across specialty testing, clinical trials and women's health, strengthen customer relationships and enhance the company’s ability to generate long-term growth.

2

Drive personalized health solutions:

•Expanded the Labcorp OnDemand test menu, including the launch of our Advanced Health Panel and the Marker by Labcorp™ Genetic Health Panel. The OnDemand portfolio now has over 140 panels and tests.
•On Labcorp OnDemand, launched the Thrive 5 Health Test in collaboration with Thrive Global that combines lab results with other health biomarkers and personalized recommendations to improve health.

Shape our future through technology and innovation:

•Announced enhancements to Labcorp Global Trial Connect™, including new capabilities across the Labcorp Sponsor™ and Labcorp Investigator™ portal experiences.


Labcorp also remains committed to a disciplined allocation of capital. In the third quarter of 2026, the company invested $255.5 million in acquisitions and paid out $58.2 million in dividends. On October 21, 2026, the company announced a quarterly cash dividend of $0.72 per share of common stock, payable on December 11, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 24, 2026. The company has $1.4 billion remaining under its board-authorized share repurchase program, providing continued flexibility to return capital to shareholders.

About Labcorp
Labcorp (NYSE: LH) is a global leader of innovative and comprehensive laboratory services that helps doctors, hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, researchers, and patients make clear and confident decisions. We provide insights and advance science to improve health and improve lives through our unparalleled diagnostics and drug development laboratory capabilities. The company's nearly 71,000 employees serve clients in approximately 100 countries, provided support for more than 85% of the new drugs and therapeutic products approved by the FDA in 2025, and performed more than 750 million tests for patients around the world. Learn more at www.labcorp.com.
