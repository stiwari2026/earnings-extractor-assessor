SAN DIEGO, CA — Nov. 3, 2026 — First Tracks Biotherapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: TRAX), a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing antibody therapeutics that modulate immune pathways implicated in autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“ANB033, a subcutaneously administered CD122 antagonist designed to inhibit signaling of both IL-15 and IL-2 on immune cells, demonstrated statistically significant protection against gluten-induced intestinal damage and symptoms and was well tolerated in the Phase 1b gluten-challenge cohort. These data support our plans to progress ANB033 into late-stage development in 2027 in celiac disease as a potential treatment to prevent both acute symptoms and intestinal damage from gluten exposure despite general adherence to a gluten-free diet,” said Daniel Faga, president and chief executive officer. “We will continue to work with regulators, clinicians and the patient community to advance ANB033. We anticipate initiating Phase 2 development in vitiligo and one additional indication in the first half of 2027, and reporting top-line results from the celiac disease mucosal-healing cohort in the first quarter of 2027 and in eosinophilic esophagitis in the third quarter of 2027.”

•

LS mean change from baseline in intraepithelial lymphocyte (IEL) density of -1.20 on ANB033 vs. +18.83 on placebo, a -20.03 placebo-adjusted delta (p<0.001)

▪
Well tolerated with no safety signals observed

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
The separation of AnaptysBio, Inc. and First Tracks Biotherapeutics, Inc. was completed on April 20, 2026. As a result, for the period from January 1, 2026 to April 19, 2026 and for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, the financial results include assets, liabilities, and expenses related to both companies. The combined financial statements reflect allocations of certain expenses from the financial statements of AnaptysBio, Inc. including research and development expenses and general and administrative expenses. These allocations include, but are not limited to, executive management, employee compensation and benefits, facilities and operations, information technology, business development, financial services (such as accounting, audit, and tax), legal, insurance, and stock-based compensation.
•
Research and development expenses were $23.8 million and $83.4 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $33.3 million and $114.1 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily due to decreased development costs for rosnilimab and ANB032, offset by increased costs relating to the Phase 1 trial for ANB033.
•
General and administrative expenses were $9.0 million and $37.1 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $11.7 million and $28.2 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was due to the allocation of legal costs for the separation of the company from AnaptysBio, Inc. and AnaptysBio, Inc.’s lawsuit with GSK and Tesaro.
•
Net loss was $31.4 million and $115.2 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, or a net loss per share of $0.88 and $3.25, respectively, compared to a net loss of $41.9 million and $131.5 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, or a net loss per share of $1.50 and $4.52.

 

About First Tracks Biotherapeutics

First Tracks Biotherapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing antibody therapies that modulate immune pathways implicated in autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Its pipeline includes ANB033, a CD122 antagonist in development for celiac disease and eosinophilic esophagitis; rosnilimab, a pathogenic T cell depleter in development for rheumatoid arthritis; and ANB101, a BDCA2 modulator. To learn more, visit www.FirstTracksBio.com or follow us on LinkedIn.
