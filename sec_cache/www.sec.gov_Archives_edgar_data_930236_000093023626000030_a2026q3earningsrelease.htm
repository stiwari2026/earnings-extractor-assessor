REDWOOD TRUST REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS AND ANNOUNCES PLANNED TRANSITION TO A C-CORPORATION
MILL VALLEY, CA – Redwood Trust, Inc. (NYSE:RWT; "Redwood," the "Company"), a leader in expanding access to housing for homebuyers and renters, today reported its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Strategic Update
•In a separate press release issued today, Redwood announced its plan to transition to a C-corporation in 2027 to align corporate strategy with business structure and enhance shareholder value
•The transition is expected to result in an initial $0.73–$0.77 per share increase in GAAP book value(1) in the fourth quarter of 2026, while providing greater flexibility to retain and reinvest earnings. The full announcement is available on Redwood’s website at https://www.redwoodtrust.com/investor-relations/news-events/press-releases

•Separately, the Company announced that it has upsized its secured financing facility with CPP Investments and Stone Point Credit, extending the maturity date to March 2030 and further strengthening financing flexibility
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Book value performance reflects Core Segment profitability, significant progress resolving assets in the Legacy Investment Segment, and dividends paid amid market volatility
◦GAAP book value per common share declined 4.9% to $6.56 at September 30, 2026, compared to $6.90 per share at June 30, 2026
◦Consolidated GAAP net loss was $(0.39) per basic and diluted common share. Non-GAAP Earnings Available for Distribution ("EAD")* was $(0.02) per share, and Non-GAAP Core Segments EAD* was $0.18 per share
▪The difference between consolidated GAAP results and EAD primarily reflects investment fair value changes related to dispositions within Legacy Investments
•Mortgage Banking(2) platforms maintained attractive returns while accelerating pipeline distribution. These platforms generated a 26% annualized non-GAAP return on capital ("ROC")*, their ninth consecutive quarter above 20%
◦Mortgage Banking production of $6.6 billion, compared to $8.0 billion in the second quarter 2026
▪Mortgage Banking platforms generated GAAP net income of $27.0 million, as compared to $40.1 million in the second quarter 2026
▪Distributed $8.8 billion of loans across our Mortgage Banking platforms through securitizations, whole loan sales and joint ventures, significantly reducing inventory exposure(3)
•Maintained strong liquidity through proactive financing and distribution initiatives. Redwood ended September with $201 million of cash and cash equivalents and warehouse line utilization of 37% of total capacity(4)
•Technology investments delivered measurable productivity gains and positive investment returns. Continue to drive AI adoption across the organization, with AI-enabled automation now generating approximately 28,300 hours of annualized time savings, a 20% increase from the second quarter. Additionally, RWT Horizons' venture investment portfolio contributed $4.9 million of
1
positive fair value changes during the quarter, driven by unrealized gains and monetizations across our Real Estate Technology and AI investments
Q4 2026 Business Priorities
•Accelerate Legacy Investment resolutions to improve GAAP earnings. Capital allocated to Legacy Investments has declined approximately 75% over the past year to $116 million at September 30, 2026. Additional resolutions are expected during the fourth quarter, with the objective of materially reducing the associated earnings and financing burden beginning Q1 2027
•Prioritize corporate deleveraging while positioning for a lower long-term cost of capital. Opportunistic repurchases of unsecured debt at an attractive discount will be prioritized, while reshaping overall unsecured debt capital structure over time as obligations become callable
•Renew foundation for organic book value growth. Greater earnings retention, reduced cash income taxes, continued Legacy Investment resolutions and disciplined expense management are expected to support more stable growth beginning in Q1 2027, without relying on a recovery in mortgage market activity
“The third quarter of 2026 was among the most challenging for our industry since the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic. We prioritized liquidity while maintaining strong returns in mortgage banking,” said Christopher Abate, Chief Executive Officer of Redwood Trust. “We distributed $8.8 billion of mortgage loans during the quarter, while continuing to serve our seller network. In aligning our corporate structure with our business model going forward, we can leverage our competitive advantages and continue growing market share—a major strategic priority as housing inventory for sale hits multi-year highs and the need for purchase-money mortgages potentially rises."

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
To supplement consolidated and segment financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP"), the Company also provides the following non-GAAP measures:
•Earnings Available for Distribution (“EAD”)
•EAD Return on Equity ("EAD ROE")
•EAD per Basic Common Share
•Core Segments Earnings Available for Distribution (“Core Segments EAD”)
•Core Segments EAD Return on Equity ("Core Segments EAD ROE")
•Core Segments EAD per Basic Common Share
•Mortgage Banking Return on Capital ("ROC")
•Redwood Investments Recourse Leverage Ratio
•Legacy Investments Recourse Leverage Ratio
•Recourse Leverage Ratio
Management believes these non-GAAP measures provide useful supplemental information to investors and management in evaluating the Company’s operating performance, facilitating comparisons to industry peers, and assessing the current income-generating capacity of the Company’s operating platforms as of the period presented. These measures also assist in evaluating the Company’s ongoing transition to a more mortgage banking focused business model, including the wind-down of legacy portfolio holdings within the Legacy Investments segment.
These non-GAAP measures should not be utilized in isolation, nor should they be considered as an alternative to GAAP net income (loss) available (related) to common stockholders, or other measurements of results of operations computed in accordance with GAAP or for federal income tax purposes.
EAD, EAD ROE and EAD per Basic Common Share
EAD, EAD ROE and EAD per basic common share are non-GAAP financial measures that the Company has historically reported and continues to use to present management’s non-GAAP analysis of the operating performance of the Company’s different business segments.
EAD is defined as GAAP net income (loss) available (related) to common stockholders, adjusted to (i) exclude investment fair value changes, net; (ii) exclude realized gains and losses; (iii) exclude acquisition-related expenses; (iv) exclude certain organizational restructuring charges, as applicable; and (v) reflect a hypothetical income tax adjustment associated with these items.
EAD ROE is defined as EAD divided by average common equity.
EAD per basic common share is calculated as EAD divided by the basic weighted average number of common shares outstanding during the period.
Core Segments EAD, Core Segments EAD ROE and Core Segments EAD per Basic Common Share
Core Segments EAD, Core Segments EAD ROE and Core Segments EAD per basic common share are non-GAAP measures and are used to present management’s non-GAAP analysis of the combined performance of the Company’s mortgage banking platforms and related investments (which are defined as the "Core Segments" and which consist of the Company’s Sequoia Mortgage Banking, Aspire Mortgage Banking, CoreVest Mortgage Banking and Redwood Investments segments), inclusive of an allocated portion of the Company’s Corporate segment relating to those Core Segments. Core Segments EAD excludes the Company’s Legacy Investments segment and excludes an allocated portion of the Company’s Corporate segment relating to the Legacy Investments segment.
12
Core Segments EAD is defined as: GAAP net income (loss) available (related) to common stockholders adjusted to (i) exclude GAAP net loss from the Legacy Investments Segment, (ii) exclude the portion of the Corporate Segment allocation relating to the Legacy Investments segment, (iii) exclude investment fair value changes, net; (iv) exclude realized gains and losses; (v) exclude acquisition-related expenses; (vi) exclude certain organizational restructuring charges (as applicable); and (vii) adjust for the hypothetical income taxes associated with these adjustments.
Core Segments EAD ROE is calculated as Core Segments EAD divided by the average capital utilized by the Core Segments during the period, which represents management’s internal estimate of the average economic capital allocated to support Core Segments activities. Average capital utilized by the Core Segments was $738 million and $737 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively.
Core Segments EAD per basic common share is calculated as Core Segments EAD divided by the basic weighted average number of common shares outstanding during the period.
Mortgage Banking Return on Capital ("ROC")
ROC for the combined Mortgage Banking platforms is a non-GAAP measure calculated as annualized net income for the Company’s combined Mortgage Banking platforms, divided by the average capital utilized by the combined Mortgage Banking platforms for the period. Average capital utilized represents management’s internal estimate of the average capital deployed to support the activities of these platforms and was $422 million and $485 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively.
Redwood Investments Recourse Leverage Ratio
Redwood Investments recourse leverage ratio is defined as recourse debt at Redwood Investments divided by capital invested. At September 30, 2026 recourse debt excludes $24.3 billion of consolidated securitization debt (ABS issued and servicer advance financing), other liabilities and other debt at Redwood Investments that is non-recourse to Redwood. Capital invested in our Redwood Investments segment at September 30, 2026 was $875 million.
Legacy Investments Recourse Leverage Ratio
Legacy Investments recourse leverage ratio is defined as recourse debt at Legacy Investments divided by capital invested. At September 30, 2026 recourse debt excludes $239 million of consolidated securitization debt (ABS issued), other liabilities and other debt at Legacy Investments that is non-recourse to Redwood. Capital invested in our Legacy Investments segment at September 30, 2026 was $116 million.
Recourse Leverage Ratio
Recourse leverage ratio is defined as recourse debt divided by capital invested. At September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026 recourse debt excludes $25.1 billion and $23.4 billion, respectively, of consolidated securitization debt (ABS issued), other liabilities and other debt that is non-recourse to Redwood. Capital invested at September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026 was $803 million and $904 million, respectively.

About Redwood Trust
Redwood Trust, Inc. (NYSE: RWT) is a specialty finance company focused on several distinct areas of housing credit where we provide liquidity to growing segments of the U.S. housing market not well served by government programs. We deliver customized housing credit investments to a diverse mix of investors, through our best-in-class securitization platforms, whole-loan distribution activities, joint ventures and our publicly traded shares. We operate through three core residential housing-focused operating platforms — Sequoia, Aspire, and CoreVest — alongside our complementary Redwood Investments portfolio which is primarily composed of assets we source through these platforms. Redwood Investments also includes RWT Horizons®, our unified technology platform spanning internal AI innovation and strategic investments across the ecosystem, which supports our efforts to develop an AI-first operating model that enables compounding operational leverage and scalable growth. This reflects how we manage and organize our business and may differ from the manner in which our reportable segments are presented for financial reporting purposes.

Our goal is to provide attractive returns to shareholders through earnings and dividends, capital appreciation, and a commitment to technological innovation that facilitates risk-minded scale. Redwood Trust is internally managed and is currently structured as a real estate investment trust ("REIT") for tax purposes. The Company has announced a plan to transition to a C-corporation beginning with its 2027 tax year. For more information about Redwood, please visit our website at www.redwoodtrust.com or connect with us on LinkedIn.
